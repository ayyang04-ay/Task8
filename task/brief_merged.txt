[REV] P4 WAS WRONG FOUR TIMES AND IS NOW MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSERTED. FINAL FORM 2026-11-13. The original 1.504x spread took the IL-2 panel's plateau for its zero-dose arm; the true arms give 2.333x. That figure is arithmetically right and analytically wrong, because the four arms are four different conditions: the with-TNF record carries TNF in every well including its zero arm, and the IL-2 record's zero point is a titration floor, so most of the apparent spread is an IL-2 dose effect. Pooling them is the cross-record move the golden rejects elsewhere. The third attempt, 1.227x from records 2 and 6, is a between-study offset across different donor sets, and setting it against a within-donor paired effect mixes designs: section 6 states each donor preparation was divided across all of the conditions in its own study, so pairing controls for donor and run. The underlying reason all three failed is the same. There is no replicate of any single condition anywhere in the package, so the data cannot speak to run-to-run variability at all. That is a property of the room, not of the wording. The fourth error was mine and was the opposite of the first three. Having rejected the records 2 and 6 comparison as a between-study offset, I then wrote that no condition is measured more than once, which is false. Three records carry the same untreated arm: nondiabetic donors, freshly isolated CD4, 48 hours, nothing added, on the stated 200 U/ml background. P4's final form uses that replicate, in the one way the comparison is legitimate. Baseline against baseline under the same stated condition is how non-comparability is established; endpoint against endpoint across records whose baselines differ is the error that follows from ignoring it. The replicated arm reads 10.622 plus or minus 0.706 at n of 13 against 8.660 plus or minus 0.598 at n of 8, a difference of 1.962 points, SE 0.925, z = 2.12, two-sided p = 0.034. It also rests on the mounted contradiction: section 4 of the companion document states that all cell culture experiments included low-level IL-2 at 200 U/ml, while record 5 titrates added IL-2 from zero and reads 7.064 percent at its 200 U/ml arm against record 2's vehicle at 10.622 percent. Those cannot both describe the same base condition, so no cross-record comparison of the asset against another expansion agent is supported. The within-record effect is not in question and never was. Record 2's 1.3038x is paired within donor at n=13 and clears noise even unpaired, Welch t = 2.51 from the tabulated means and SEMs.
VERIFIED AT BUILD: the 1.082x bound. Re-digitised at 300, 400 and 600 dpi; it is stable to plus or minus 0.001. Golden states it as a band of 1.00 to 1.09, never a point value, and notes that any lag pushes it toward 1.00, and that it bounds composition only if total CD4 is non-decreasing.

### Named domain knowledge, marked as such in the golden
Human conventional CD4 T cells transiently induce FOXP3 on activation, so bulk FOXP3 in activated CD4 is not Treg-specific. THIS IS THE LEG A REVIEWER WILL CHALLENGE; the room does not carry it.
Tregs consume rather than produce IL-2, so rising IL2 transcript is not a Treg signature.
Activated T cells do not complete a first division within 10 h. Supports but is NOT required by the mounted kinetics bound.
The lineage-defining transcription factors absent from the panel: TBX21, GATA3, RORC, BCL6.
