Advance TRN-416, but advance it as a humanisation and candidate-selection programme rather than as a molecule ready for IND-enabling toxicology, and strike the per-cell potency claim from the development position until the studies below read out: the package shows the antibody expands a FoxP3-positive population and shows the binding article is clean, it does not show that the cells it produces are better than conventionally expanded Tregs or that the expanded population is Treg at all, and the article every one of those measurements was made on is a murine hybridoma product that cannot itself be the clinical candidate.

THE CASE

The expansion is real within a record. In TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx the CD25-high FoxP3-positive fraction runs from 10.622 percent at 0 ug/ml to 13.849 percent at 2.5 ug/ml across thirteen donor preparations, a factor of 1.3038 taken as the ratio of the two tabulated means. The curve is dose-ordered through the mid-range and plateaus above 2.5 ug/ml. Frequency alone would not establish expansion, because a frequency can rise when the denominator falls, but TRN416_treg_absolute_counts.xlsx gives absolute counts on the same dose series at the same 48 hour timepoint, 2029 cells at 0 ug/ml and 2965 cells at 2.5 ug/ml across eleven preparations, a factor of 1.4613. Both move in the same direction, so the effect is a real increase in cell number and not a compositional artefact of the denominator. It also replicates: the 0 to 2.5 ug/ml change is plus 3.227 points in the nondiabetic record, plus 3.466 in the T1D record and plus 4.053 in the record carrying added TNF, which pool to plus 3.451 points with a standard error of 0.669, z of 5.16, and a Cochran Q of 0.099 on 2 degrees of freedom. Three records, three conditions, no detectable heterogeneity in the effect. With three studies the between-study variance cannot be estimated with any precision, so this establishes consistency of direction rather than a magnitude.

The records cannot be lined up against each other, and the reason is not simply run-to-run noise. The zero-dose arms read 10.622 percent in TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx, 10.290 percent in TRN416_flow_treg_dose_with_tnf.xlsx, 8.660 percent in TRN416_flow_treg_tnf_dose.xlsx and 4.553 percent in TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx, but these are four different conditions, not one condition measured four times. The with_tnf record carries TNF at 20 ng/ml in every well including its zero-dose arm, per its own metadata and section 6.3 of the companion document, so its 10.290 is a TNF-containing condition. The il2_dose record's zero point is the bottom of an IL-2 titration, so its 4.553 is a low-IL-2 condition, and the bulk of the apparent 2.3330 spread against the control-donor record is an IL-2 dose effect rather than assay variability. Pooling the four into a single variability estimate would be the same cross-record move this analysis rejects elsewhere, so I do not make it. The records can be shown not to share a scale, rather than merely asserted not to. One untreated condition is replicated: nondiabetic donors, freshly isolated CD4, 48 hours, nothing added, on the stated 200 U/ml background. TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx reads 10.622 plus or minus 0.706 at n of 13 and TRN416_flow_treg_tnf_dose.xlsx reads 8.660 plus or minus 0.598 at n of 8, a difference of 1.962 points with a standard error of 0.925, giving z of 2.12 and a two-sided p of 0.034. The same stated condition differs detectably between records. This is a comparison across records, which this analysis refuses everywhere else, and the distinction matters: comparing two baselines measured under the same stated condition is how non-comparability is established, whereas comparing endpoints across records whose baselines differ is the error that follows from ignoring it. None of this bears on whether the within-record effect is real. Section 6 states that each donor preparation was divided across all of the conditions in its own study, so the 1.3038 factor is a within-donor paired effect and a between-study baseline offset does not threaten it. What the incommensurability forecloses is narrower and it is the thing that matters here: the package cannot support any comparison of this asset against another expansion agent, because the records that would supply the comparison do not share a baseline condition. Section 4 of TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf states that all cell culture experiments included low-level IL-2 at 200 U/ml, while TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx titrates added IL-2 from 0 U/ml and then sits flat at 7.06 to 7.11 percent across every non-zero dose including 200 U/ml. The document and that record cannot both be describing the same base condition. No comparison of TRN-416 against IL-2, against TNF, or against any other expansion agent is supported by this package, and any claim of superiority drawn from the endpoint percentages is comparing incommensurable baselines.

The expression data describe an acute response, not the expanded population. TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx was taken at 10 hours, per section 9.1 of the companion document, while every expansion readout is at 48 hours. Taking the count change as exponential from time zero, the growth rate is the natural log of 2965 over 2029 divided by 48 hours, which is 0.007903 per hour. Carried forward to 10 hours that gives a composition multiplier of at most 1.082. I state this as a band of 1.00 to 1.09 rather than a point value, for two reasons: both inputs are summary means, and any lag before the first division pushes the figure toward 1.00, which is conservative in the direction that matters here. The bound is a ceiling on composition only if total CD4 is non-decreasing across the interval. The two records together recover relative total CD4 at 48 hours but not its direction from time zero, so that assumption has to be stated and cannot be checked from the package. Against that ceiling, the paired within-donor geometric mean for FOXP3 of 2.8383 means at least 2.62 of it, the ratio 2.8383 over 1.082, is transcriptional and cannot be accounted for by a change in which cells are in the well. The 10 hour data therefore report what receptor ligation does acutely. They are not a characterisation of the cells present at the 48 hour endpoint, and nothing in the package bridges the two timepoints.

The compartment cannot be assigned, and I am not going to resolve it. TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx is bulk sequencing of a CD4 preparation, and section 3.1 of the companion document gives the preparation as immunomagnetic negative selection to greater than 95 percent CD4 purity. Treg frequency in the untreated condition is 10.622 percent. If the whole FOXP3 change sits in Tregs, each Treg has to raise FOXP3 by a factor of 2.84. If it sits in the conventional cells instead, they have to reach a fraction of the per-Treg FOXP3 level given by 2.8383 minus 1, times the Treg fraction, divided by one minus the Treg fraction. That fraction is not measured in the sequenced material. The expression study ran at IL-2 50 U/ml in 2 percent serum in 24-well plates, per section 9.1, while the 10.622 percent comes from a 200 U/ml, 10 percent serum, 96-well study, and the companion document reports CD4 purity for the sequenced material but no Treg frequency for it. Carrying 10.622 percent across gives 21.8 percent, but the honest statement is a range: at a Treg fraction of 5 percent the figure is 9.7 percent and at 20 percent it is 46 percent. The point does not depend on which value is right. Across that whole range both branches remain feasible. Both are physiologically feasible and the package contains nothing that separates them. This matters because, as named external knowledge not carried by these files, human conventional CD4 T cells transiently induce FOXP3 on activation, so bulk FOXP3 in an activated mixed CD4 culture is not a Treg-specific readout and the flow gate on CD25-high FoxP3-positive events does not exclude activated conventional cells.

Per-cell suppressive capacity is unmeasured. TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx reads 83.66, 73.01, 64.09 and 49.41 percent CD8 division at responder-to-Treg ratios of 1:0, 4:1, 2:1 and 1:1, with n of 7. That establishes the added cells suppress and that suppression tracks dose. It does not establish potency per cell, because the metadata field for added cells states that every ratio received CD4 cultures expanded with TRN-416 at 2.5 ug/ml. The only thing that varies across the four arms is how many cells were added. The 1:0 arm contains no added cells at all and is a zero point, not a comparator for cell quality. Nothing in the package compares a TRN-416-expanded Treg against a conventionally expanded one.

The effectors move against the identity markers. Under paired within-donor analysis, IL10 is 0.1819, IL4 is 0.2280, CTLA4 is 0.8558 and PDCD1 is 0.7760, each down in 12 of 12 donors, exact two-sided sign test p of 0.0005. TGFB1 is 1.0300 at 8 of 12, p of 0.3877, and is not supported. The transcripts that carry suppressor function fall while the identity transcripts rise. That is not a contradiction of the identity signal, but it is the opposite of what a potency claim built on this panel would need. CD3G falls 0.7453-fold in 12 of 12 donors on the same paired analysis, which is consistent with T cell receptor complex downmodulation on activation and so with the broad-activation reading rather than with a clean Treg program. Both observations sit inside one record and need no comparison across records.

Analysis has to be paired. Section 9.1 of the companion document states that each donor preparation was split into two wells, so the design is paired. The values are already normalized and are non-integer, so renormalization is neither available nor needed, and a pooled or between-group test is the wrong estimator. Under paired analysis three of the headline results do not survive. ETS1 is 1.0703 at 9 of 12, p of 0.1460, against a pooled ratio of 1.3526. IL7R is 0.5184 at 7 of 12, p of 0.7744, against a pooled 0.7036. MDH2 reverses outright: pooled 1.2187 against paired 0.8537 at 1 of 12, p of 0.0063, with the reversal carried by a single donor, DN-04, at 7.394. The absolute induction criterion stated in section 9.4, an induction of at least 1200 normalized reads, admits 13 of the 70 transcripts. Applied consistently it excludes GLS2 at 3.0508 in 12 of 12 donors, CPT1B at 1.2696 in 12 of 12 and IL2 at 1.8958 in 12 of 12, whose mean absolute inductions are 17.5, 16.9 and 8.4 reads. It admits exactly one transcript that the paired test rejects, and that transcript is ETS1, which carries the largest absolute induction on the panel at 16132 reads while failing the sign test. The criterion and the paired test disagree on one gene and it is a headline gene.

The panel cannot settle the question. The 70 transcripts in TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx contain no lineage-defining transcription factor of a competing CD4 lineage and no dedicated proliferation readout. MYC is present and is proliferation-associated, but it is not a proliferation marker in the sense this question needs; establishing division requires a dye-dilution assay such as CellTrace Violet or a conventional marker such as Ki-67, and the package has neither. IKZF2 is the one Treg-associated discriminator present, and it is not sufficient on its own. As named external knowledge, the transcription factors that would be needed are TBX21, GATA3, RORC and BCL6, and none is present. Without them the data cannot distinguish a Treg program from broad activation, and without a proliferation marker they cannot report on division at all. Note also, as named external knowledge, that Tregs consume IL-2 rather than produce it, so the IL2 transcript rise of 1.8958 in 12 of 12 donors argues against a pure Treg response rather than for one.

The molecule is not what is in question. TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx gives a global-fit K_D of 1.2 nM with a standard error of 0.2 nM over a six-point analyte series from 50 to 1.5625 nM, consistent per-concentration KD values and chi-squared below 0.12, on a 100 RU capture against a 26 RU theoretical maximum with double referencing. TRN416_elisa_receptor_counterscreen.xlsx gives an OD450 of 2.541 against the TNFR2 ectodomain at 10 ug/ml and 0.062 against TNFR1 at the same concentration, where the bovine serum albumin well reads 0.048, so TNFR1 binding is at blank. TRN416_epitope_mapping_clips.xlsx localises binding to three discontinuous sequences. In TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx the F(ab')2 fragment reaches 13.37 percent at 0.91 ug/ml across five preparations, so activity does not require the Fc region; the cross-linking arm runs 9.24 to 12.91 percent across three preparations, though every well in that record contains the cross-linking reagent and it carries no zero-cross-linker arm, so it establishes a dose response in the presence of cross-linker and cannot speak to whether cross-linker is required; sTNFR2 in supernatant runs 111.2, 205.9 and 323.7 pg/ml for IL-2, TNF and TRN-416 at ten preparations each; and the NF-kB array shows 28 of 39 genes above the IL-2-only wells at six hours with n of 7, the largest being TANK at 2.683. Format, selectivity and proximal signalling are all clean. That is what keeps the programme alive.

The article all of that was measured on cannot be the clinical candidate. Section 1 of TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf gives TRN-416 as a mouse IgG1 kappa raised in a BALB/c hybridoma, against a recombinant fragment of human TNFR2 spanning amino acids 50 to 122, purified on protein A. The detection chemistry across the package corroborates it rather than relying on that one line: the receptor counter-screen, the epitope mapping and the cross-linking arm all use anti-rodent or anti-mouse immunoglobulin secondaries, and the cross-linking reagent is itself an anti-rodent IgG. A murine hybridoma product cannot be dosed chronically in an autoimmune indication, so whatever enters IND-enabling work is a humanised or otherwise engineered derivative. The package contains no such derivative. There is no mention anywhere in it, in the companion document or in any data file, of humanisation, chimerisation, CDR grafting or a human framework. Every figure in the preceding paragraph is therefore a property of a research reagent rather than of a candidate. That is not a reason to discount any of it: the binding, the selectivity and the Fc independence are real results and they are what make the engineering worth funding. It does mean each has to be re-established on whatever molecule is taken forward. One consequence runs in the programme's favour: because the F(ab')2 fragment retains activity, the engineering has latitude on the Fc and is not obliged to preserve a murine effector function that was never carrying the effect.

THE STUDIES

One concurrent expansion experiment. It has to establish whether the effect exceeds standard expansion and whether it exceeds run-to-run variability. Comparators, all in a single run on the same donors: unstimulated, vehicle, IL-2 plus TCR stimulation, and a standard-of-care Treg expansion protocol, against TRN-416. This is necessitated by the fact that no two records in the package share a baseline condition, and by the package contradicting itself about what that condition was: section 4 states that all cell culture experiments included low-level IL-2 at 200 U/ml, while record 5 titrates added IL-2 from zero. It is also necessitated by the limits of what the package can show. The one untreated condition that is replicated is replicated across studies on different donor sets, so it measures between-study and between-donor variation together and cannot isolate assay drift. Only a single run carrying all the arms on one donor set can establish whether the effect exceeds standard expansion. Until it runs, no claim that TRN-416 outperforms anything is defensible.

Paired mixed and isolated culture arms with single-cell sequencing, including a timepoint at or past the expansion endpoint. It has to establish which compartment responds, whether the response is a Treg program or broad activation, and what the contrast between direct and indirect action is. Comparators: the within-run arms above, and critically the mixed culture against the isolated one. This is necessitated by the 1.082 composition ceiling and the 10.622 percent Treg frequency, which together make the current bulk data unassignable. Sorting alone is not the remedy. Mixed culture is the physiologically relevant condition, so running only sorted Tregs discards the question rather than answering it. The contrast between the two arms is the readout.

Treg identification carrying discriminators beyond FOXP3. It has to establish that the population being scored is Treg and not activation-induced FoxP3-positive conventional cells. The comparator is the current FOXP3-alone gate, which is the thing being replaced. This is necessitated by the external fact above about activation-induced FOXP3. The panel is not empty on this point: IKZF2, which encodes Helios, is present and moves 2.8931-fold in 12 of 12 donors, and Helios is used as a discriminator beyond FOXP3. But a transcript is not a gate, Helios as a Treg-specific marker is contested, and IKZF4 and a demethylation readout are both absent, so the package cannot settle it either.

Matched-ratio suppression. It has to establish per-cell suppressive capacity. Comparator: TRN-416-expanded Tregs against conventionally expanded Tregs from the same donor, at identical ratios, read out the same way. This is necessitated by the arm list in TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx, in which no non-TRN-416 Treg arm exists.

Durability and stability. It has to establish whether the induced state persists after withdrawal, and whether the cells drift. Comparator: cells held in continuous exposure against cells withdrawn from the agonist, the same donor preparation in both, read at matched timepoints past 48 hours. Withdrawal against continued exposure is the contrast; exhaustion readouts are part of the readout rather than the comparator. This is necessitated by the fact that no result beyond 48 hours is tabulated anywhere. The cross-linking study states that 24, 48 and 72 hours were examined, but only the 48 hour timepoint is reported, so there is no readout in the package on which persistence could be judged, and no withdrawal or washout design at all.

Humanisation and candidate selection. It has to establish that an engineered derivative retains the binding, selectivity and functional profile of the research reagent, and it has to produce the molecule the rest of the programme is run on. Comparators: the murine parent at matched concentrations, read out on K_D, the TNFR1 counter-screen, the epitope, and the expansion assay. This is necessitated by section 1 of TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf and by the absence of any humanised or chimeric form anywhere in the package. This item is on the critical path, because until it reads out every binding and potency figure above describes a reagent rather than a candidate.

Mechanism. It has to establish how receptor ligation produces the phenotype, which the NF-kB array at six hours gestures at without resolving. Comparator: receptor-engaged against receptor-blocked cells from the same donor preparation, since the NF-kB array as mounted contrasts only treated against IL-2-only wells. This is necessitated by the panel being unable to discriminate, and it is the lowest priority of the seven because nothing else waits on it.

WHAT I LOOKED AT AND SET ASIDE

TRN416_flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors.xlsx, the T1D donor dose-response at n of 11. It shows the same dose-ordered rise, 10.902 percent at 0 ug/ml to 14.368 percent at 2.5 ug/ml. It is consistent with the control-donor record and supports indication relevance, but it carries the same compartment and scale problems and adds no independent evidence on either, so it does not change the position.

The itaconate sheet of TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, reading 614601, 642714 and 700373 scaled intensity at 0, 0.5 and 2.5 ug/ml. It is dose-ordered and it is a supernatant measurement at six hours on a different cell input. It cannot speak to the identity of the expanded population, which is the open question, so I did not build on it.

The TNF and IL-2 dose-response records as comparators for TRN-416. Set aside for the scale reason given above. They remain useful as evidence that the assay responds to known agents, and I used them only for that.

Two data-quality items, flagged and not pursued. TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx contains two rows for BDNF carrying different values, and IL2RA is missing both arms for one donor, leaving it at n of 11 where every other transcript is at 12. Neither touches any number above: BDNF is not load-bearing, and I report IL2RA at its true n. These look like transcription artefacts in the export rather than anything meaning-bearing, and I would ask the client to confirm rather than treat them as evidence of a problem with the data set.

Several inconsistencies between the companion document and the data files, flagged and not pursued. IL-2 is given at 200 U/ml in general culture and 50 U/ml in the expression study; normalization scope is given as per-subject in section 9.4 and per-sequencing-site in section 9.3; the suppression responders are described as T1D donors and the sample count as normal blood donors; the sTNFR2 agonist dose is 12.5 ug/ml in the document and 2.5 ug/ml in the data file; the itaconate n is 6 in one line and 12 in another; and the itaconate dose series carries four doses in the document against three in the data file. The first two are load-bearing and I have used the scope stated in section 9.4, per-subject, because that is the scope attached to the normalization step itself and it is also the one the paired design requires. The rest do not affect any figure I have relied on. I would want all of them reconciled before an IND-enabling package is assembled, but none of them is a reason to stop the programme here.

As a final note on method: every figure-derived value in this response comes from the tabulated summary records, so the ratios carry the precision of those summaries and not more. Where I give a ratio I have given the two numbers it came from and the file they sit in, so each one can be checked.
