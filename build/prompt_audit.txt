THE REMOVED TOXICOLOGY SLOT: CONSIDERED AND RESOLVED, NO CHANGE NEEDED
Removing the allocated slot removes any cost to deferring, so a non-committal answer becomes defensible. This was raised as a risk and is not one. Three checks settle it.
First, the brief's own hedge mode is defined as recommended advancement without naming studies or comparators, foreclosed by the fact that the ask requires studies with comparators. The mode is about failing to name studies, not about failing to commit to a verdict, and the ask carries it without help from the scene.
Second, the grader does not grade the verdict. No A item requires any particular direction. The single B item touching the recommendation penalises recommending a direction without naming the studies that support it and what they are compared against, which is the same foreclosure. C explicitly neutralises the wording of the recommendation.
Third, none of the other seven expected-to-fire modes depends on a forcing event in the scene: timepoint conflation, compartment assignment, comparator blindness, cross-run comparison, pooled estimator, sort-only remedy and the FOXP3 gate are all foreclosed by mounted facts.
The prompt-craft rule that the scene is where deferral is made expensive is conditional on deferral being an attractive wrong answer. It is not wrong here. The golden's verdict is advance with the per-cell potency claim struck until the studies read out, which is a near neighbour of declining to commit, and the brief states that the verdict carries no credit. The rule's precondition does not hold, so the line stays out.

WHAT TO WATCH IN THE PANEL INSTEAD
The live risk in the studies ask is not hedging. It is a plausible study list produced from general drug-development instinct without touching a single mounted number: run a head-to-head, add single-cell sequencing, use better Treg markers, run matched-ratio suppression, check durability, work out mechanism. That list is most of the golden and is reachable without doing any of the pillar work.
It is caught by the grader rather than by the prompt. A1 to A10 require the derivations and A15 requires the file and the reduction behind every number, so a study list with no numbers under it fails on content even where the studies themselves look right. When reading trajectories, check whether the study list is anchored to the mounted findings that necessitate it or floats free of them.
