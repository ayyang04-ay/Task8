THE ONE SUBSTANTIVE RISK
Removing the toxicology slot removes the cost of deferring. The scene no longer contains an event that happens whether or not a recommendation is made, so "we cannot tell from this package, go and run the studies first" is now a fully defensible answer that also satisfies the studies ask without committing to a verdict.
The pre-registered mode this bears on is the unspecified hedge. That mode is foreclosed by the ask rather than by the scene, because the prompt requires studies with named comparators and a hedge that names none fails it. So the mode survives. What is lost is the second guard, and the risk is a panel that returns analytically strong answers with weak or absent verdicts.
Against that: the brief states that the verdict carries no credit and all credit is on the pillars, and every pillar is still elicited. A weak verdict costs the task less than it would if the verdict were graded.
Recommendation: restore a cost to deferral in one line, without naming a specific slot, since naming it was what made the sentence awkward under the client framing. Something that states when the client has to decide and what happens if the answer is late. If the slot stays out, expect hedged verdicts in the panel and treat them as a scene artefact rather than as a failure mode.
