RECOMMENDATION: Do not commit to IND-enabling work on TRN-416. Hold the asset at lead optimisation, stop generating more of the same assay, and fund a short, tightly controlled confirmation block first. TRN-416 is a good, well-characterised, on-target binder — but the package contains no controlled demonstration of agonist pharmacology, three of its datasets fail internal arithmetic, and the molecule is still an unhumanised murine hybridoma IgG1 with no tox-species cross-reactivity data, so there is no IND candidate to enable yet.

All file names below are in the client package directory. "PDF" = TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf (TRN-DOC-0416-07 Rev 3).

=====================================================================
1. WHAT THE PACKAGE GENUINELY ESTABLISHES (and it is not trivial)
=====================================================================

1.1 Receptor selectivity over TNFR1 is well supported.
TRN416_elisa_receptor_counterscreen.xlsx, sheet "counterscreen" (study BP-010). I took the raw OD450 values and subtracted each antigen's own 0 ug/ml row (TNFR2 0.047, TNFR2 aa50-122 0.046, TNFR1 0.048, BSA 0.043). At the 10 ug/ml top dose the blank-subtracted signals are TNFR2 2.494, TNFR2 aa50-122 2.142, TNFR1 0.014, BSA 0.005. Ratio 2.494/0.014 = 178-fold.
Stronger than the ratio: I computed the blank-subtracted TNFR1 series across the whole 8-point, ~2000-fold titration and it DECREASES monotonically (0.014, 0.011, 0.010, 0.007, 0.006, 0.004, 0.003, 0.002, 0.000). There is no dose-dependence, i.e. no detectable TNFR1 binding at all. 178-fold is a detection floor, not a measured ratio. Design is sound: triplicate wells, three independent runs, a protein (BSA) and a no-primary blank on every antigen.

1.2 Affinity is credible and the kinetic table is internally self-consistent.
TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx, sheet "kinetics" (BP-009). Reported global KD 1.2 +/- 0.2 nM. I recomputed KD = kd/ka for all six analyte concentrations: 0.000579/482000 = 1.20e-9; 0.000573/477000 = 1.20e-9; 0.000588/491000 = 1.20e-9; 0.000571/468000 = 1.22e-9; 0.000564/474000 = 1.19e-9; 0.000582/480000 = 1.21e-9. Every reported KD reproduces exactly from its own rate constants, and chi-squared is 0.076-0.118 RU^2 against a 23-26 RU signal (<0.5%). Capture level is tight (99.5-101.4 RU across six cycles), so the surface is stable. Orientation is correct for an intrinsic affinity measurement (antibody captured, monomeric receptor ectodomain as analyte).

1.3 The epitope is mapped to discrete, reproducibly-ranked sites, and that map is corroborated by an independent assay.
TRN416_epitope_mapping_clips.xlsx (BP-011). Three site clusters sit far above background: QMCCSKCSPGQH (res. 44-55) 2874 AU, CSSDQVET (98-105) 2655 AU, NRICTCRP (116-123) 2413 AU, versus scrambled control 96 AU and three non-binding linear peptides 143-188 AU. Signal-to-background 2874/96 = 30-fold. Each cluster shows the shoulder pattern you expect from a real tiled map (e.g. 97-104 = 884, 98-105 = 2655, 99-106 = 1411 — the signal peaks and falls off as the core is walked out of frame), which is hard to fake and hard to get from a sticky antibody.
Independent corroboration: the immunogen fragment aa 50-122 (PDF s.1) spans two of the three cores, and in BP-010 that fragment alone retains 2.142/2.494 = 86% of the full-ectodomain signal. Two unrelated assays agree on where the antibody binds.

1.4 Binding and function line up — the modest cell effect is probably on-target.
This is the best argument the client has and they do not make it. I fitted a 4-parameter logistic to the baseline-subtracted means:
  - Functional EC50, TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx (FC-002): 0.232 ug/ml = 1.55 nM.
  - Functional EC50, TRN416_flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors.xlsx (FC-003): 0.324 ug/ml = 2.16 nM.
  - Binding EC50, BP-010 ELISA, full ectodomain: 0.331 ug/ml = 2.21 nM (Hill 0.93).
  - SPR KD, BP-009: 1.2 nM = 0.180 ug/ml at 150 kDa.
Four independent measurements inside a 2.1-fold window. A non-specific or endotoxin-driven effect would not track the binding curve this closely. Potency is also the same in T1D and control donors (1.55 vs 2.16 nM), which matters for the indication.

1.5 There is a coherent, mechanistically-correct second-messenger signature.
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet "nfkb_array" (AR-012D), relative quantitation vs paired IL-2-only wells: TRAF3 at or below the 0.75 reporting floor (down), MAP3K14/NIK 1.628, NFKB2 1.877, RELB 2.573, BIRC3 2.099. That is the canonical non-canonical (NIK/NF-kB2/RelB) axis that TNFR2 is supposed to drive, and it is the right direction for every member. It survives correction for the plate's own reference genes (RPLP0 1.124, HMBS 1.086, mean 1.105): RELB becomes 2.33, NFKB2 1.70, MAP3K14 1.47.

1.6 The cell effect is not purely a gating/denominator artefact.
TRN416_treg_absolute_counts.xlsx (FC-007) reports absolute CD25hi FoxP3+ cells per well, 2029 at vehicle to 2965 at 2.5 ug/ml = 1.461-fold (+936 cells). An increase in the absolute count cannot be produced by the denominator shrinking, so something real is happening to cell number as well as to percentage.

=====================================================================
2. WHY THAT IS STILL NOT ENOUGH TO SPEND IND-ENABLING MONEY
=====================================================================

2.1 THE DECISIVE NUMBER: the drug effect is smaller than the scatter of the vehicle control between experiments.
PDF s.4 states that every culture in the programme contained IL-2 at 200 U/ml, so the "no test article, 48 h, freshly isolated CD4+" well is the same condition in five different studies. I pulled that well from each file:
  - FC-002 (TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx), 0 ug/ml:        10.622% (SEM 0.706, n=13)
  - FC-003 (TRN416_flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors.xlsx), 0 ug/ml:            10.902% (SEM 0.481, n=11)
  - FC-004 (TRN416_flow_treg_dose_with_tnf.xlsx), 0 ug/ml (+TNF 20):    10.290% (SEM 1.12, n=3)
  - FC-006 (TRN416_flow_treg_tnf_dose.xlsx), 0 ng/ml TNF:                8.660% (SEM 0.598, n=8)
  - FC-005 (TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx), IL-2 200 U/ml:              7.064% (SEM 0.298, n=7)
Spread of the same nominal condition: 10.902 - 7.064 = 3.838 percentage points.
Now the claimed drug effect, computed as max mean minus the 0 ug/ml mean in the same file:
  - FC-002: 14.101 - 10.622 = +3.479 pp (1.328-fold)
  - FC-003: 14.866 - 10.902 = +3.964 pp (1.364-fold)
  - FC-004: 14.936 - 10.290 = +4.646 pp (1.452-fold)
The pharmacology (3.5-4.6 pp) is the same size as, or smaller than, the between-experiment drift of the untreated control (3.84 pp). For comparison, the within-experiment donor SD in FC-002 is SEM x sqrt(n) = 0.706 x sqrt(13) = 2.546 pp. So the plate-to-plate bias (3.84) EXCEEDS the biological donor variation (2.55). Consequence: nothing in this package can be interpreted except against an on-plate control — and the two studies that are supposed to carry the mechanism have no on-plate control at all (see 2.3).

2.2 There is no isotype control anywhere in the package, and there is a specific reason to want one.
I grepped the PDF and every sheet for "isotype", "control antibody", "irrelevant" — the only hit is the PDF describing TRN-416's own isotype (murine IgG1 kappa, s.1). Not one experiment includes an isotype-matched, endotoxin-matched murine IgG1.
This is not box-ticking. Release spec is "below 1 EU/mg" (PDF s.1), i.e. up to 0.025 EU/ml at the 25 ug/ml top dose. And the client's own expression data show exactly the signature trace endotoxin would give: in TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx I ran paired t-tests across the 12 donors and got IL1B 2.16-fold up (p=0.010, up in 12/12 donors), TNF 1.33-fold up (p=0.0009, 12/12), IL2 1.62-fold up (p=0.00014, 12/12), CD274/PD-L1 3.27-fold up (p=0.00076, 12/12). Without an endotoxin-matched IgG1 arm, an innate-contaminant explanation for the whole cell package cannot be excluded.
Also unrecorded: three lots were used (TRN416-L07/L09/L11, PDF s.1) and no file says which lot went into which study, so the baseline drift in 2.1 cannot be attributed or ruled out as a lot effect.

2.3 The two studies that exist to prove the agonist mechanism are unusable, and they contradict each other.
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet "fab2_fragment" (FC-012A, n=5). Three of the ten rows are arithmetically impossible. For non-negative data (a percentage cannot be negative) with sample size n, the largest achievable SEM equals the mean itself: the extreme configuration is one value at n x mean and the rest at zero, which gives SD = mean x sqrt(n) and therefore SEM = mean. Check:
  - 0 ug/ml:      mean 0.16, SEM 0.55  -> maximum possible SEM 0.16. IMPOSSIBLE.
  - 0.001 ug/ml:  mean 0.16, SEM 0.51  -> maximum possible 0.16. IMPOSSIBLE.
  - 0.0073 ug/ml: mean 0.84, SEM 0.88  -> maximum possible 0.84. IMPOSSIBLE.
Those are precisely the three low-dose rows that manufacture the headline: a vehicle baseline of 0.16% rising to 13.37%, an 84-fold effect. Independently, that 0.16% baseline is impossible on its own terms — the same cells, media and 48 h incubation give 7.06-10.90% in all five studies in 2.1. I would set the whole F(ab')2 table aside.
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet "cross_linking" (FC-012B, n=3). No 0 ug/ml well exists — the table starts at 0.0008 ug/ml — so the experiment cannot answer the only question it was run to answer ("does cross-linking increase the response?"). SEM is missing from 4 of 8 rows (0.004, 0.02, 0.5, 25 ug/ml). And on the numbers that are there, cross-linking adds nothing: taking the lowest common dose as reference, cross-linked goes 9.24 -> 12.14 at 2.5 ug/ml (+2.90 pp) while plain IgG in FC-002 goes 11.042 -> 13.849 (+2.81 pp). Identical.
Taken together the mechanism arms are incoherent. For a TNFRSF agonist the expected potency/efficacy order is cross-linked IgG > IgG > F(ab')2. What the files show is F(ab')2 apparently MORE potent than IgG (EC50 ~0.1 ug/ml by eye vs 0.232 ug/ml fitted for IgG) and cross-linking adding nothing — and all three formats converging on the same 12.9-14.1% ceiling, which points at the culture setting the ceiling rather than the drug.

2.4 Agonism is never compared with the natural ligand, and when you do compare across files it looks no better than TNF.
TRN416_flow_treg_tnf_dose.xlsx (FC-006): TNF alone, 0 -> 100 ng/ml, 8.660 -> 11.438 = +2.778 pp, 1.321-fold. TRN-416's own maximum (FC-002) is 1.328-fold. The antibody reproduces recombinant TNF's amplitude to three decimal places in fold terms. The entire commercial rationale for an agonist antibody over TNF is a better effect or a cleaner one; no experiment in the package puts TNF and TRN-416 in the same plate, so that rationale is untested.
Worse, the one study that mixes them argues against a shared receptor. FC-006 shows TNF at 20 ng/ml already delivers +2.158 pp of its own +2.778 pp maximum (78% of TNF's Emax). FC-004 adds TRN-416 on top of TNF 20 ng/ml in every well and still gets +4.646 pp — a LARGER increment than TRN-416 gives alone (+3.479 pp), with no occlusion whatsoever. If both agents engaged TNFR2, a near-saturating ligand concentration should compress the antibody's increment. It does not. (Caveat: FC-004 is n=3 with SEMs up to 2.076, and the two studies are on different plates, which 2.1 says is exactly the comparison you cannot trust — which is the point: the client never ran the on-plate matrix.)

2.5 The entire Treg-selectivity claim rests on a population that was gated and never reported.
PDF s.5 defines the Teff gate ("CD25 high and FoxP3 negative, assessed on the same plot"). I searched every sheet of all 12 workbooks: there is no Teff column, row or sheet anywhere. So the client measured effector T cells in the same plots, in the same wells, and the package reports only the Treg number.
This is the single most important omission. "Treg % of CD4+" cannot distinguish Treg expansion from Teff contraction, and TNFR2 is expressed on activated effector T cells too — so an agonist that expands Teff alongside Treg is the central safety hazard of the whole target class in autoimmunity. The endpoint that matters is the Treg:Teff ratio, and it is absent. The flow panel also carries no viability dye (PDF s.5: CD25-PE in FL2 and FoxP3-AF488 in FL1 only; viability is trypan blue on a hemocytometer), so dead-cell artefacts in the FoxP3 intracellular stain are not excluded either.

2.6 The suppression study has no comparator, and only one of its three ratios is even nominally significant.
TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx (FC-008, n=7). The sheet states the added cells are "CD4+ cultures expanded with TRN-416 2.5 ug/ml for 48 h, ALL ARMS" — so every arm got TRN-416-expanded bulk CD4 cells and there is no arm with vehicle-expanded or isotype-expanded cells. The design therefore measures the effect of adding more CD4 cells, not the effect of the drug. There is no cell-number control (e.g. irradiated or third-party cells at matched numbers), so the fall in CD8 division from 83.66% at 1:0 to 49.41% at 1:1 is fully confounded with competition for the soluble anti-CD3 and the IL-2 at 50 U/ml. The added cells are also bulk CD4 cultures, not sorted Tregs — at 1:1 that is 50,000 bulk CD4 cells, of which roughly 14% are Treg on the client's own FC-002 numbers.
On the reported summaries (Welch t-test from the mean/SEM pairs, n=7 per arm): 1:0 vs 4:1 p=0.426; 1:0 vs 2:1 p=0.214; 1:0 vs 1:1 p=0.036. Only the most confounded ratio clears 0.05, and only just.
The PDF also contradicts itself and the sheet on this study: PDF s.8 says responders were "CD8+ T cells from donors with type 1 diabetes" and then "Samples (n) 7, from normal blood donors"; the sheet says "Autologous CD8+ T cells". And the suppression-index formula in PDF s.8 is not a formula: "(Treg cell proliferation without Treg - Tresp proliferation with Treg) / Tresp proliferation without Treg cell" mixes Treg and Tresp in the numerator. No suppression index is actually tabulated anywhere.

2.7 The RNA-seq study has verbatim duplicated donor data and an invalid significance rule. I would not use it.
TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx.
Hard duplicates (these are not rounding coincidences):
  - SUCLG1 reads exactly (3146.2, 1669.82) for the IL-2-only / IL-2+TRN-416 pair in THREE different donors, DN-01, DN-05 and DN-09.
  - SUCLG1 IL-2-only is exactly 1687.41019185791 in both DN-03 and DN-11 — identical to 15 significant figures.
  - CREBBP IL-2-only is exactly 2529.87 in both DN-01 and DN-05.
  - IDH3G reads exactly (631, 468) in DN-08 and DN-12; PDCD1 exactly (57, 34) in DN-03 and DN-07.
Structural defects: 71 gene rows for 70 declared transcripts (PDF Appendix A) because BDNF appears twice with different values; IL2RA is blank in both DN-07 columns with no missingness declared; 12 cells carry 12-15 significant figures (MYC DN-04 5851.89326902868, PDCD1LG2 DN-04 52.0079388120819, SLC3A2 DN-04 2187.23657666668) while the rest are integers, which is the fingerprint of values inserted by hand or imputed rather than produced by one pipeline.
Implausible homogeneity: I computed per-donor direction of change for every row. 50 of 71 rows move the same way in all 12 donors — including unrelated glycolytic enzymes (ALDOA down in 12/12, TPI1 12/12, PKM 12/12, ENO1 12/12). In the client's own flow assays the donor-to-donor CV is roughly 7-24% (SEM/mean), so zero directional heterogeneity across 50 of 70 transcripts in primary human CD4 cells is not credible.
The significance rule is not a statistical test: PDF s.9.4 defines significance as "an induction of at least 1200 normalized reads". That is an absolute count difference, with no fold change, no variance, no test and no multiple-testing correction, so only highly expressed genes can ever qualify. I re-ran it properly (paired t-test per gene, Benjamini-Hochberg across 71 rows). The two criteria barely agree: 13 genes pass the client's 1200-read rule; 47 genes reach q<0.05 that the rule misses entirely (GLS2 3.07-fold, q=7.1e-6; IL10 0.20-fold, q=0.00027; IL4 0.22-fold, q=0.0006; KEAP1 0.58-fold, q=4.1e-5); and ETS1 passes the client's rule (+16,132 reads) while the paired test gives p=0.19 because it is bimodal (down in DN-03/07/11, up in the other nine).
Methods contradictions in the same study: PDF s.9.1 says "the incubation time for the samples reported here was 10 hours" while the s.9.2 table says "Harvest time after addition of test article: 1h". PDF s.9.1 uses 2% FBS with IL-2 at 50 U/ml, contradicting s.4's "all cell culture experiments included low-level IL-2 at 200 U/ml" in 10% FBS. Libraries were built at two sites with two different kits (NEBNext Ultra vs Ultra II) and "normalized for each sequencing laboratory separately" (s.9.3/9.4) — and the data file records no site assignment, so the batch structure cannot even be inspected. Note that the two bimodal genes, IL7R (down in DN-06/07/09/11/12, up in the rest, p=0.18) and ETS1, split the donors in a way that looks like exactly that unrecorded site split.
Finally, 70 transcripts are reported out of a whole-transcriptome run with no indication of how they were chosen and no full dataset. The same applies to AR-012D: 39 genes reported from a 96-well TaqMan plate.

2.8 The IL-2 control curve is not real data.
TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx (FC-005). Across six IL-2 doses spanning 2500-fold (2 to 5000 U/ml) there are only THREE distinct mean values (7.064, 7.085, 7.106) and TWO distinct SEMs (0.298, 0.319), total span 0.042 pp. The sequence is 7.106, 7.106, 7.085, 7.064, 7.085, 7.106 with SEMs 0.319, 0.319, 0.298, 0.298, 0.298, 0.319. That is a copy-paste pattern, not seven donors measured at six concentrations. Which is a problem, because this is the study that was supposed to prove the 200 U/ml IL-2 background in every other well is not driving the readout.

2.9 The SPR table cannot be what it says it is.
TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx. It declares "Fit model: 1:1 Langmuir, global" and "Theoretical Rmax at this capture level: 26 RU", then reports a separate "Rmax observed" and a separate ka/kd/KD for each of the six analyte concentrations. A global fit returns one shared ka, one kd and one Rmax by construction, so per-concentration values contradict the stated model.
Read instead as measured response, the column contradicts the client's own KD. With Rmax 26 RU and KD 1.2 nM, steady-state response R = 26 x C/(C + 1.2) gives expected 25.39, 24.81, 23.72, 21.81, 18.79 and 14.71 RU at 50, 25, 12.5, 6.25, 3.125 and 1.5625 nM. Observed: 25.6, 25.1, 24.7, 24.2, 23.8, 23.1. The two top points agree (ratio 1.01) and then the table diverges progressively: 1.11x at 6.25 nM, 1.27x at 3.125 nM and 1.57x at 1.5625 nM. At 1.56 nM the surface is reported 57% more occupied than a KD of 1.2 nM permits. Either the KD or that column is wrong.
Two further method problems: dissociation was followed for 10 minutes (PDF s.10) but kd = 5.79e-4 /s gives a complex half-life of ln(2)/5.79e-4 = 1197 s = 20.0 minutes, so only half a half-life was observed — kd is extrapolated, and kd is what sets KD here. And PDF s.10 states the antibody was "injected at a concentration of 1 ng/ml" (6.7 pM) to reach 100 RU of capture, which is not achievable in a normal injection.

2.10 The ancillary endpoints will not support the weight put on them.
sTNFR2 shedding, TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx sheet "stnfr2_supernatant" (EL-012C). Reported means: IL-2 alone 111.2 pg/ml, TNF 20 ng/ml 205.9, TRN-416 323.7. That is 323.7/111.2 = 2.91-fold over IL-2 and 1.57-fold over TNF. No SD, SEM or p-value is given for any arm, so none of these differences is testable. And the assay cannot be trusted as run: the analyte is soluble TNFR2, captured on "commercial plates and custom plates coated with 2 ug per well anti-TNFR2", in a supernatant that contains micrograms per millilitre of an anti-TNFR2 antibody. No spike-recovery or interference control is reported. TRN-416 will compete with or complex the capture reagent, and the direction of the artefact is unknown. Note also that PDF s.13.3 says the TRN-416 arm was dosed at 12.5 ug/ml while the data sheet says 2.5 ug/ml.
Separately: if the 2.9-fold is real, the client has filed a liability under a positive heading. Soluble TNFR2 is a TNF decoy and its appearance means target is leaving the cell surface. Nobody has looked at surface TNFR2 by flow.
Itaconate, sheet "itaconate_supernatant" (MB-012E). Reported means 614,601 / 642,714 / 700,373 scaled intensity at 0 / 0.5 / 2.5 ug/ml, i.e. a 1.14-fold change, with SEMs of 1850 / 2400 / 1100. Converting to SD at the stated n=6 gives coefficients of variation of 0.74%, 0.91% and 0.38% ACROSS SIX DIFFERENT HUMAN DONORS on an outsourced mass-spec platform. That implies t = 39.9 for the 0 vs 2.5 comparison. For scale, the same lab's flow assays show donor CVs of 7-24%. These dispersions are not believable. The record is also broken: PDF s.13.5 lists four doses (0, 0.1, 0.5, 2.5) and the sheet has three — 0.1 ug/ml is missing; PDF s.13.5 says "Samples (n) 12" in one line and "n = 6 donors" two lines earlier, and the sheet says 6. And itaconate has no mechanistic anchor anywhere else in the package: ACOD1/IRG1 is not among the 70 transcripts in Appendix A, so nothing connects this endpoint to the asserted biology.
NF-kB array (AR-012D), beyond 1.5 above: no SD, SEM or p-value column exists; every IL-2-only value is set to exactly 1.000, which erases the control's variability entirely; the two reference genes are themselves shifted 8.6% and 12.4% (HMBS 1.086, RPLP0 1.124), so the whole panel is displaced; and four genes are censored at the 0.75 reporting floor (MAP3K1, MAP3K12, TNFAIP3, TRAF3) — including TRAF3, which is the mechanistically load-bearing one. The panel also skews upward overall (median RQ 1.225, 28 of 39 genes above 1.0), consistent with a normalisation offset rather than 28 real inductions.

2.11 The two flow studies disagree with each other by a factor of ten on cell number.
FC-002 reports 10.622% Treg at vehicle; FC-007 reports 2029 Treg cells per well at vehicle. PDF s.4 says wells were seeded at 0.2-1 x 10^6 CD4+ cells. 2029 / 0.10622 = 19,102 total CD4 cells recovered, which is 9.6% of the lowest seeding density and 1.9% of the highest. PDF s.3.1 states viability above 92% at release. Both datasets cannot be right unless more than 90% of the cells were lost, which is undeclared. The two studies also give different fold-changes for the same drug (1.328-fold on percentage, 1.461-fold on count), implying a ~10% drug effect on total CD4 number that is never reported.

2.12 No p-value in the package is reproducible.
PDF s.14 claims linear mixed-effects modelling with participant-level random effects for the dose-response trend, paired t-tests for expression, ANOVA with Tukey for multi-condition comparisons. Donor-level data exist for exactly one study (RS-001, the one I would discard). Every flow, ELISA, suppression, sTNFR2, array and metabolite dataset is summary-only. No mixed model can be fitted, no paired test can be run, and no reported statistic can be checked by anyone.

2.13 There is no IND candidate, and no tox species.
PDF s.1: TRN-416 is a murine IgG1 kappa from a BALB/c hybridoma, Protein A purified. It has not been humanised, germlined, de-risked for immunogenicity, or had an Fc decision taken — and for a TNFRSF agonist the Fc (isotype, FcgR engagement, hexamerisation) is a primary efficacy determinant, not a formulation detail. No file contains cross-reactivity to cynomolgus, mouse or rat TNFR2, so no pharmacologically relevant tox species has been identified. No developability data beyond ">95% by SDS-PAGE and SEC": no aggregation, forced degradation, viscosity, thermal stability or expression data. Lead optimisation is not finished; IND-enabling tox on this molecule would be spent on a reagent that cannot become the clinical candidate.

=====================================================================
3. WHAT WOULD BRING THIS BACK, AND THE WORK THAT WOULD SHOW IT
=====================================================================
Four things have to be true. Study 1 is the gate: it is cheap, it is fast, and if it fails the asset stops. Studies 2-4 are only funded if 1 passes.

STUDY 1 — THE GATING EXPERIMENT. One plate, one donor cohort, every arm side by side.
Must establish: that TRN-416 raises the Treg:Teff ratio in human CD4+ cells, against an on-plate isotype control, by a margin larger than the 3.84 pp control drift measured in 2.1.
Design: freshly isolated CD4+ from >=12 nondiabetic and >=12 T1D donors (matching FC-002/FC-003), full 10-point TRN-416 curve, all arms on the same plate on the same day, with a viability dye and CD127 added to the panel.
Comparators, all four mandatory and all on the same plate:
  (a) Isotype-matched murine IgG1, same lot history, endotoxin matched to the TRN-416 lot by LAL — this is the primary comparator and it does not exist anywhere in the current package (2.2).
  (b) Vehicle (IL-2 200 U/ml alone) in the same plate — the only valid baseline given 2.1.
  (c) Recombinant human TNF across its own full curve, in the same plate — the natural-ligand benchmark, never run head-to-head (2.4).
  (d) A full IL-2 titration in the same plate, to replace the unusable FC-005 (2.8).
Endpoints: CD25hi FoxP3+ %, CD25hi FoxP3- (Teff) %, the Treg:Teff ratio, absolute counts of both by bead-based counting (not hemocytometer, per 2.11), and total viable CD4 recovery.
Deliverable: donor-level data, not means and SEMs, so the mixed model in PDF s.14 can actually be fitted (2.12).
Pass criterion to set in advance: a Treg:Teff ratio increase over on-plate isotype that is significant on a paired model and whose lower confidence bound exceeds the historical control drift. If TRN-416 only matches isotype, or only moves Treg% without moving the ratio, or only matches TNF, the asset stops here.

STUDY 2 — MECHANISM AND VALENCY. Replaces FC-012A and FC-012B, which are unusable (2.3).
Must establish: that the response is TNFR2-dependent and that the valency requirement is understood well enough to choose a clinical format.
Arms in one experiment, each with its own vehicle well: intact IgG; F(ab')2; monovalent Fab or F(ab'); IgG plus anti-murine-IgG cross-linker; cross-linker alone; isotype in each format. Full curves, n>=6 donors, 24/48/72 h.
Comparators: on-plate vehicle and on-plate isotype in the matched format (a F(ab')2 arm must be compared against isotype F(ab')2, not against IgG from another plate).
Dependence proof, which no current study attempts: repeat the TRN-416 curve in (i) TNFR2-blocked cells using a non-competing blocking antibody or soluble TNFR2 ectodomain as a sink, and (ii) TNFRSF1B-knockdown or -knockout primary CD4 cells, against isogenic non-targeting controls. Loss of effect in both is the test.
Occlusion matrix: TNF x TRN-416 full 2-D matrix in one plate, to settle whether the non-occlusion seen across FC-004 and FC-006 (2.4) is real or a plate artefact. If TRN-416's effect is preserved at saturating TNF and survives TNFR2 knockout, the effect is off-target and the asset stops.

STUDY 3 — FUNCTION. Replaces FC-008 (2.6).
Must establish: that TRN-416-conditioned Tregs suppress better per cell, not just that adding cells crowds a well.
Design: FACS-sorted CD4+CD25hiCD127lo Tregs (not bulk CD4 cultures), expanded with TRN-416 or isotype, titrated against CFSE-labelled autologous CD8+ and CD4+ responders at matched Treg input numbers, >=8 donors each of nondiabetic and T1D origin, with anti-CD3 and IL-2 held constant.
Comparators: (a) isotype-expanded sorted Tregs at identical cell numbers — the comparator that is missing; (b) a cell-number-only control (irradiated or third-party cells at matched numbers) to separate suppression from crowding and IL-2 competition; (c) TNF-expanded Tregs; (d) a responder-only arm.
Report a defined suppression index, since the formula in PDF s.8 is not computable and no index is tabulated anywhere.

STUDY 4 — THE SAFETY QUESTIONS THAT DECIDE WHETHER AN AGONIST IS DEVELOPABLE AT ALL.
(a) Effector activation. Whole-blood and PBMC cytokine release assay, soluble and plate-immobilised format, against a known superagonist positive control and an isotype negative control; plus CD8+ and CD4+ Teff proliferation and effector cytokines under TRN-416. This is the first-in-human gating risk for any TNFR2 agonist and the package contains nothing on it.
(b) Target shedding. Surface TNFR2 by flow over 0-72 h alongside supernatant sTNFR2, using a capture antibody with an epitope that does not overlap the three cores mapped in BP-011, and with spike-recovery of recombinant sTNFR2 into TRN-416-containing medium to quantify the interference in 2.10. Comparators: isotype, TNF, and TRN-416-spiked cell-free medium.
(c) Broader specificity. The ELISA counter-screen (1.1) covers one off-target. Needed: the full TNFRSF panel by SPR or ELISA, plus a membrane-proteome or cell-microarray specificity screen, plus tissue cross-reactivity on a human panel. Comparator: isotype on the same arrays.
(d) Tox species and candidate format. Cyno, mouse and rat TNFR2 binding by SPR and ELISA against human, benchmarked to the human KD of 1.2 nM, and epitope conservation across the three mapped cores. Then humanisation, Fc selection and a developability package. Until a cross-reactive species exists, no GLP tox study can be designed.

REMEDIATION THAT IS NOT OPTIONAL, WHATEVER THE SCIENCE SHOWS.
Before any of the above is credited, ask the client for: raw FCS files and donor-level values for every flow study; raw FASTQ or count matrices, per-sample RIN, and the sequencing-site assignment for RS-001 (2.7); the full 96-gene output and Ct values for AR-012D; the Metabolon raw report for MB-012E; sensorgrams and the BIAevaluation fit files for BP-009 (2.9); the lot-to-study assignment and LAL certificates for L07/L09/L11. Then a written explanation of the three impossible SEMs in FC-012A (2.3), the duplicated donor values in RS-001 (2.7), and the three-distinct-values IL-2 table in FC-005 (2.8). If those cannot be reconciled from source records, the problem is the quality system rather than the molecule, and the diligence conclusion changes from "not yet" to "not from this group".

USED AS EVIDENCE, weighted highest to lowest.
TRN416_elisa_receptor_counterscreen.xlsx (BP-010) — highest confidence. Proper blanks on every antigen, a protein control, triplicate wells in three independent runs, monotonic 8-point titration, and a second antigen (aa 50-122) that independently corroborates the epitope map. Carries 1.1 and 1.4.
TRN416_epitope_mapping_clips.xlsx (BP-011) — high confidence on location. Scrambled control plus three non-binding linear peptides give a real background, and the tiling shoulders behave correctly. Caveat I noted but did not treat as disqualifying: the tabulated "peptide sequences" do not match the construct lengths declared in PDF s.12 — single-loop entries are 8 residues against a stated 17-mer, double-loop entries are 12 residues against a stated 22-mer, and the three "15-mer linear" entries are 16 residues (and their residue spans, e.g. 29-44, confirm 16). These read as annotated epitope cores rather than the constructs assayed. It is a QC failure in the record, not a reason to doubt where the antibody binds.
TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx (BP-009) — used for the affinity magnitude only (1.2, 1.4), not as a validated kinetic dataset, for the reasons in 2.9.
TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx, _t1d_donors.xlsx, _tnf_dose.xlsx, _dose_with_tnf.xlsx (FC-002/003/004/006) — used for effect size, potency and the control-drift argument (2.1, 2.4, 1.4). Not used to support an efficacy claim, because none has an isotype arm.
TRN416_treg_absolute_counts.xlsx (FC-007) — used for the direction-of-effect point (1.6) and for the internal inconsistency in 2.11.
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx sheet nfkb_array (AR-012D) — used for mechanistic plausibility only (1.5), because it has no dispersion, no statistics, a shifted reference-gene pair and a censoring floor on the key gene.
TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf — used throughout for design, conditions and the internal contradictions; it is the only source for the IL-2 200 U/ml background (s.4), the Teff gate (s.5), the induction criterion (s.9.4), the molecule's species and isotype (s.1) and the statistical claims (s.14).

SET ASIDE, with reasons.
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx sheet fab2_fragment (FC-012A) — discarded entirely. Three of ten rows carry SEMs that exceed the arithmetic maximum for non-negative data, and they are the three rows that create the headline effect; the 0.16% vehicle baseline is irreconcilable with the 7.06-10.90% obtained in five other studies under the same conditions (2.3).
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx sheet cross_linking (FC-012B) — discarded as uninterpretable. No vehicle well, SEM missing from half the rows, n=3 (2.3).
TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx (FC-005) — discarded as a control dataset, kept only as the 200 U/ml data point in the drift analysis. Three distinct means and two distinct SEMs across six doses spanning 2500-fold is a copy-paste artefact (2.8).
TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx (RS-001) — discarded pending raw data. Verbatim duplicate donor values to 15 significant figures, a duplicated BDNF row, undeclared missing cells, mixed numeric precision, 50 of 71 rows with zero donor heterogeneity, an unmodelled two-site batch structure, and an induction criterion that is not a statistical test. I re-analysed it properly anyway (paired t-tests, BH FDR) solely to show that the client's own rule does not agree with any test, and to surface the IL1B/TNF/IL2/CD274 pattern in 2.2 and the CTLA4 0.86 / IL10 0.20 / TGFB1 unchanged pattern that argues against a clean suppressive-Treg program — but I would not build a claim on it.
TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx (FC-008) — set aside as evidence of function. No vehicle- or isotype-expanded comparator exists in the design, so it measures cell addition (2.6).
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx sheet stnfr2_supernatant (EL-012C) — set aside as efficacy evidence, retained as a liability flag requiring the interference control in Study 4b (2.10).
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx sheet itaconate_supernatant (MB-012E) — set aside. Inter-donor CVs of 0.38-0.91% on an outsourced mass-spec platform across six donors are not credible, a declared dose is missing from the sheet, the n is stated three different ways, and the endpoint has no mechanistic anchor elsewhere in the package (2.10).
