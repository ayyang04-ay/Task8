PART 1 — ERRORS, MOST SERIOUS FIRST
=====================================================================

FINDING 1 (MOST SERIOUS). The headline 2.3330 "spread in the no-drug condition" is built on a
mischaracterisation of three of its four inputs. Three of the four values are NOT the
"no-drug, no-added-agent condition."

The analysis: "The no-drug, no-added-agent condition reads 10.622 percent in
..._dose_control_donors.xlsx, 10.290 percent in ..._dose_with_tnf.xlsx, 8.660 percent in
..._dose_tnf_dose.xlsx and 4.553 percent in ..._dose_il2_dose.xlsx. The spread is 10.622 divided
by 4.553, a factor of 2.3330."

What the room says:
 - TRN416_flow_treg_dose_with_tnf.xlsx cell B7: "Recombinant human TNF, 20 ng/ml, all wells"; PDF
   s.6.3: "Recombinant human TNF at 20 ng/ml was added to every well, including the well receiving
   no TRN-416." So its 0 ug/ml value (10.290) is a TNF-treated well, not a no-added-agent well.
 - TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx: the 0 U/ml row (4.553) is the ZERO point of an IL-2 titration.
   PDF s.6.4 specifies the conditions for FC-005 as "Recombinant human IL-2 added at 0, 2, 10, 50,
   200, 1000 and 5000 U/ml. No test article was added to any well." It does NOT restate the
   200 U/ml background, whereas PDF s.6.1, s.6.2 and s.6.3 each explicitly do ("Media as in
   section 4, including IL-2 at 200 U/ml in every well"). PDF s.4 ends: "Culture conditions
   specific to an individual study are given in that study's section and take precedence over this
   paragraph." On the document's own precedence rule, the FC-005 0-arm is an IL-2-free well.
 - Same for TRN416_flow_treg_tnf_dose.xlsx (PDF s.6.5, no 200 U/ml restatement).

Consequence: the "spread" is overwhelmingly an IL-2 dose effect, not run-to-run variability. The
two records that unambiguously share the section-4 media and a genuinely agent-free 0-arm are
FC-002 (10.622) and — once TNF is set aside — nothing else; FC-002 vs FC-004 differ by 3.2%
(10.622 vs 10.290), not 133%. The correct statement of inter-record baseline variability among
comparable records is a factor of about 1.03 (10.622/10.290), or at most about 1.23
(10.622/8.660) if FC-006's 0-arm is taken as comparable. 2.3330 is not a measure of assay
variability.

This is load-bearing twice over: it is the sole cited justification for Study 1 ("This is
necessitated by the 2.3330 spread in the no-drug condition across the four dose-response
records"), and it carries the paragraph's conclusion that "any claim of superiority drawn from the
endpoint percentages is comparing incommensurable baselines." The recommendation to run a
concurrent-comparator experiment is still right for other reasons (different n, different donors,
no within-run comparator), but the quantitative warrant given for it is wrong.

FINDING 2 (VERY SERIOUS). "The absence of any discriminating marker in the mounted panel" is
contradicted by the panel itself. IKZF2 (Helios) is present, is a transcription factor, and is one
of the strongest signals in the file.

The analysis asserts, in two places:
 - "The 70 transcripts ... contain no lineage-defining transcription factor of a competing CD4
   lineage and no proliferation marker."
 - Study 3 is "necessitated by ... the absence of any discriminating marker in the mounted panel."

TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx row 32 is IKZF2. Recomputed paired within-donor geometric
mean 2.8931, up in 12 of 12 donors, exact sign-test p=0.000488, mean absolute induction
2830.4 normalized reads — i.e. it also passes the s.9.4 >=1200-read criterion and is one of the
13 admitted transcripts the analysis itself counted. IL2RA (row 37, gm 3.4523, 11/11, p=0.0010,
mean induction 7158.8 reads) and TNFRSF18/GITR (row 68, gm 1.0982, 10/12, p=0.0386) are also
mounted Treg-associated markers.

So the room DOES carry a Treg discriminator beyond FOXP3, with a large, unanimous, criterion-
passing signal, and the analysis's own compartment argument ("If the whole FOXP3 change sits in
Tregs ... If it sits in the conventional cells instead") never engages it. The correct statement
is that the panel lacks a *flow-cytometric* discriminator in the gating panel (PDF s.5 is CD25/
FoxP3 only) and lacks lineage TFs of competing lineages — not that it contains no discriminating
marker at all. Study 3 remains a sensible ask; its stated necessitating evidence is false.

FINDING 3 (SERIOUS). The 21.8 percent conventional-cell figure and the 1.082 ceiling both import
numbers across records in exactly the way the analysis declares impermissible, and the 21.8 figure
is highly sensitive to that import.

The analysis states flatly: "The records do not share a scale, so the effect cannot be compared
across them" and "No comparison of TRN-416 against IL-2, against TNF, or against any other
expansion agent is supported by this package." It then:
 (a) imports the 10.622% Treg frequency from FC-002 (48 h, 96-well round-bottom, 0.2-1e6 cells/
     well, IL-2 200 U/ml, nondiabetic donors, n=13) into the compartment arithmetic for RS-001
     (10 h, 24-well, 3e6 cells/well, IL-2 50 U/ml, n=12 — PDF s.9.1); and
 (b) imports the 48-h count means from FC-007 (n=11, IL-2 200 U/ml) to bound composition change
     in RS-001 at 10 h.
No record in the room reports Treg frequency under RS-001's conditions.

Sensitivity: recomputing (2.8383-1)*f/(1-f) at the IL-2 level RS-001 actually used —
TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx reports 7.085% at 50 U/ml added (B14) — gives 14.0 percent, not
21.8 percent. At the 0-added-IL-2 value (4.553%) it gives 8.8 percent. The quoted figure is
arithmetically correct for f=0.10622 but the choice of f is unsupported, and the answer moves by
a factor of 2.5 across defensible choices from the same room. The figure should be given as a
range (roughly 9 to 22 percent) or not quoted to three significant figures.

Also in this finding: "Taking the count change as exponential from time zero, the growth rate is
the natural log of 2965 over 2029 divided by 48 hours." 2029 is the 48-hour count of the 0 ug/ml
arm (treg_absolute_counts B13), not a time-zero count. The quantity computed is the excess
drug-attributable growth-rate difference between arms, not a growth rate from time zero. The value
(0.007903/h) and the 10-h multiplier (1.082) are the right construction for a drug-attributable
composition bound, so the number survives; the stated derivation misdescribes it.

FINDING 4 (SERIOUS). The claimed contradiction between PDF s.4 and the IL-2 record is not
established, and the paragraph's strongest sentence — "The reason is stated in the package rather
than inferred" — is a non sequitur.

The analysis: "section 4 ... says that all cell culture experiments included low-level IL-2 at
200 U/ml in the media, while TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx titrates added IL-2 from 0 U/ml and
then sits flat at 7.06 to 7.11 percent across every non-zero dose including 200 U/ml. Those two
statements cannot both describe one scale."

PDF s.4 (lines 104-105) ends with an explicit precedence clause: "Culture conditions specific to
an individual study are given in that study's section and take precedence over this paragraph."
PDF s.6.4 gives FC-005's conditions and omits the 200 U/ml background that s.6.1-6.3 each state
explicitly. The two statements therefore CAN both hold: s.6.4 overrides s.4 for FC-005. There is
no contradiction to be found here, only a document that is less explicit in s.6.4 than in s.6.1.
Separately, even if a contradiction existed, it would not establish that it is "the reason" the
four tabulated 0-arms differ — the records also differ in donors, n (13/3/8/7), and added agent.
The analysis presents an inference as a statement of the package.

FINDING 5 (SERIOUS). "This is necessitated by the absence of any timepoint beyond 48 hours
anywhere in the package" is false on the face of the room.

TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet cross_linking, cell B6: "Timepoints assessed:
24 h, 48 h, 72 h" (B7: "Tabulated timepoint 48 h"). PDF s.13.2 repeats it: "Timepoints examined
24, 48 and 72 hours." PDF s.4 also states cultures were "incubated for up to 72 hours." The
analysis quotes this very sheet two paragraphs earlier for the 9.24-12.91 range, so it had the
cell in hand. The accurate statement is that no timepoint beyond 48 hours is *tabulated*; 72 h was
assessed and the data were not reported. That is a materially different — and arguably stronger —
basis for Study 5, and it is a request the client can satisfy from existing work.

FINDING 6 (SERIOUS, logic). The cross-linking inference runs backwards. "the cross-linking arm
runs 9.24 to 12.91 percent across three preparations, so activity does not require an exogenous
cross-linker."

In that record the cross-linker is present in EVERY well (xlsx cross_linking A4/B4: "Anti-rodent
IgG clone ab9165, 2.5 ug/ml"; PDF s.13.2: "with the cross-linking reagent present in every well"),
and the record has no 0 ug/ml TRN-416 row at all (lowest dose 0.0008 = 9.24%). A record in which
every well contains the cross-linker cannot show that activity does not require one; it shows at
most that adding one does not further boost the response. The claim that activity is
cross-linker-independent rests on FC-002, where no cross-linker was used — not on this record. As
written the conclusion does not follow from the cited evidence. (The 9.24 figure is also not a
vehicle baseline, as "runs 9.24 to 12.91" implies.)

FINDING 7 (MODERATE). "no proliferation marker ... without a proliferation marker they cannot
report on division at all" overstates. MYC (row 45) and CDKN1C (row 12) are both mounted
cell-cycle/growth genes. Recomputed: MYC gm 0.9345, down in 10 of 12 donors, p=0.0386;
CDKN1C gm 0.6945, down in 11 of 12, p=0.0063. A significant MYC decrease at 10 h is directly
relevant to the analysis's own 1.082 composition ceiling — it argues the proliferative program is
not engaged at 10 h, which strengthens the conservative end of the 1.00-1.09 band. The panel does
lack a canonical cytometric proliferation marker (no MKI67, no CCNB1, no PCNA), which is the
defensible version of the claim.

Relatedly: "The 70 transcripts ... contain no lineage-defining transcription factor of a competing
CD4 lineage" is true for TBX21/GATA3/RORC/BCL6 (I confirmed all four absent from the 70-gene
Appendix A list and from the file), but the panel does carry lineage-*associated* effectors with
unanimous signals: CXCR3 (Th1-associated) up gm 2.0401, 12/12, p=0.0005; IL4 (Th2) down gm 0.2280,
12/12; IL12B down gm 0.1107, 12/12. The panel is not wholly uninformative about competing
lineages, as "the panel cannot settle the question" implies.

FINDING 8 (MODERATE, fairness). IL4 is listed among "the effectors" and "the transcripts that
carry suppressor function," and its fall is recruited as evidence against a potency claim.

"Under paired within-donor analysis, IL10 is 0.1819, IL4 is 0.2280, CTLA4 is 0.8558 and PDCD1 is
0.7760 ... The transcripts that carry suppressor function fall while the identity transcripts
rise."

IL4 is a Th2 effector cytokine, not a Treg suppressive effector. Its 4.4-fold fall in 12 of 12
donors is, if anything, evidence AGAINST Th2 contamination of the response — i.e. evidence
favourable to the Treg reading the analysis is arguing against. Grouping it with IL10/CTLA4/PDCD1
inflates the count of "effectors down" from three to four and converts favourable evidence into
unfavourable. IL12B (gm 0.1107, 12/12 down) is the same case and is not mentioned at all.

FINDING 9 (MODERATE, internal contradiction). IL2 is used both ways. The analysis excludes IL2 by
the s.9.4 criterion because its mean absolute induction is only 8.4 reads ("Applied consistently it
excludes ... IL2 at 1.8958 in 12 of 12, whose mean absolute inductions are 17.5, 16.9 and
8.4 reads"), and then, three paragraphs later, relies on the same transcript's ratio as substantive
evidence: "the IL2 transcript rise of 1.8958 in 12 of 12 donors argues against a pure Treg
response rather than for one." Both cannot stand. The raw reads are 3-44 (IL2-only) against 10-55
(TRN-416) — an 8-read mean change at counts in the single and double digits. If 8.4 reads is below
the package's own threshold of meaning, it cannot carry an argument about Treg purity.

FINDING 10 (MODERATE). "the current FOXP3-alone gate" contradicts the analysis's own earlier,
correct description and the PDF. PDF s.5 (lines 119-120): "Events within the CD4+ preparation that
are CD25 high and FoxP3 positive." The analysis states this correctly earlier ("the flow gate on
CD25-high FoxP3-positive events") and then, in Study 3, calls the comparator "the current
FOXP3-alone gate." It is a two-marker gate. Minor as arithmetic, but it is an internal
contradiction in a sentence defining a proposed study's comparator.

FINDING 11 (MODERATE, omission that cuts against the recommendation). The analysis's central
recommendation — "take the toxicology slot," justified by "Format, selectivity and proximal
signalling are all clean. That is the reason to spend the slot" — never mentions that the test
article is an unmodified murine antibody.

PDF s.1: "Species of origin: Mouse. Hybridoma raised in BALB/c." "Isotype: IgG1, kappa."
Purification is Protein A; the ELISA and CLIPS secondaries are anti-mouse/anti-rodent IgG. There is
no humanisation, no species cross-reactivity data, no in vivo data of any kind, and no toxicology
in the room (PDF s."Studies covered" lists only in vitro FC/EL/AR/MB/RS/BP studies). For a
recommendation that turns on spending an IND-enabling toxicology slot, the molecule's murine
origin is the single largest unaddressed development item in the package, and "the molecule is not
what is in question" / "the molecule itself is clean" is stronger than the room supports. This is
the clearest instance of evidence in the room that argues against the stated position and is not
engaged.

FINDING 12 (MINOR-MODERATE). "TNFR1 binding is at blank" overstates. In
TRN416_elisa_receptor_counterscreen.xlsx column D the TNFR1 series is 0.062, 0.059, 0.058, 0.055,
0.054, 0.052, 0.051, 0.050 against its own zero-antibody well at 0.048 (D24) — a monotone,
dose-ordered titration across all eight concentrations, 0.014 OD above its own blank at the top
dose. The BSA column (E) by contrast is essentially flat (0.048 down to 0.043). The magnitude is
trivial and the selectivity conclusion stands, but the TNFR1 signal is dose-dependent and
non-zero, not "at blank."

FINDING 13 (MINOR-MODERATE). The suppression ratios are repeated uncritically although the room
shows they do not describe Treg content. "reads 83.66, 73.01, 64.09 and 49.41 percent CD8 division
at responder-to-Treg ratios of 1:0, 4:1, 2:1 and 1:1."

TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx B4: the added material is "CD4+ cultures expanded with
TRN-416 2.5 ug/ml" — whole CD4 cultures, which by the same package's flow data are roughly 13.8%
CD25hi FoxP3+ at that dose. Responder input is 5e4 (PDF s.8); added cells are 12500/25000/50000
(column B). So the columns are responder:total-CD4 ratios; the true responder:Treg ratios are
roughly 29:1, 14:1 and 7:1. The analysis correctly flags that all four arms are TRN-416-expanded,
but then re-states the mislabelled axis as fact. Also: "That establishes the added cells suppress
and that suppression tracks dose" is asserted with no test; the record gives only means and SEMs
(9.71-11.29 on means 83.66 and 49.41). An unpaired two-sample t on the 1:0 vs 1:1 means gives
t=2.37, p~0.036 — real but marginal, and the arms are not independent of total cell density. The
word "establishes" is stronger than n=7 means with SEMs ~20% of the mean support.

FINDING 14 (MINOR). The F(ab')2 record is used as clean evidence while carrying the worst baseline
anomaly in the room — which the analysis elsewhere treats as disqualifying.
TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, fab2_fragment: the 0 ug/ml well reads 0.16% (B11), i.e.
66-fold below FC-002's 0 ug/ml of 10.622% and 28-fold below FC-005's 4.553%. The curve is also
non-monotone (peaks 13.37 at 0.91 ug/ml, then 11.55, 12.00, 9.35, 8.24) with SEMs up to 5.69.
By the analysis's own "incommensurable baselines" standard this record's scale should have been
flagged; instead "the F(ab')2 fragment reaches 13.37 percent" is quoted as if commensurate with
the 13.849% endpoint of FC-002. The Fc-independence conclusion is still supported within-record
(0.16 -> 13.37 is a large internal dose-response); the uncritical treatment is the issue.

FINDING 15 (MINOR). SPR quality is presented as uniformly clean while the one internal
inconsistency in the record is passed over. "consistent per-concentration KD values and chi-squared
below 0.12" is correct (KD 1.19-1.22 nM; chi2 0.076-0.118). But column C, "Rmax observed," drifts
monotonically with analyte concentration: 25.6, 25.1, 24.7, 24.2, 23.8, 23.1 RU across 50 to
1.5625 nM. In a 1:1 *global* fit (B19/PDF s.10) Rmax is a single shared parameter and should not
track analyte concentration; a monotone drift of that shape is the classic signature of
incomplete equilibration or a mass-transport/avidity contribution. It does not overturn a 1.2 nM
KD, but it is the one quality flag in the record and it is not mentioned.

FINDING 16 (MINOR). The NF-kB summary omits censoring. "28 of 39 genes above the IL-2-only wells"
is exactly right (recount: 28 up, 11 down, 39 genes). But column D flags four genes — MAP3K1,
MAP3K12, TNFAIP3, TRAF3 — as "at or below lower limit of reported range," all reported at the floor
value 0.750 (B9: "Lower limit of reported range 0.75"). Four of 39 values are censored, including
two core NF-kB negative regulators. Describing the array as showing "proximal signalling ... clean"
without noting that a tenth of the panel is floored is understated.

FINDING 17 (MINOR). "I report IL2RA at its true n" — the analysis never reports an IL2RA figure
anywhere. (For the record: gm 3.4523, 11/11 up, p=0.0010, mean induction 7158.8 reads — the
largest fold-change among the 13 criterion-passing transcripts.) The sentence claims a disclosure
that was not made.

FINDING 18 (MINOR). "ETS1 ... carries the largest absolute induction on the panel at 16132 reads."
Correct as the largest *induction* (16132.2). But the largest absolute *change* on the panel is
IL7R at -20569.5 reads, and ALDOA is -6447.9. Since the sentence is about magnitude ("largest
absolute induction"), it is worth stating that the largest absolute movement on the panel is a
reduction that the analysis separately finds non-significant (IL7R 7/12, p=0.7744) — a point that
would have reinforced its own argument.

FINDING 19 (MINOR). The flagged doc/data inconsistency list is presented as complete ("The rest do
not affect any figure I have relied on") but omits at least four more:
 - itaconate doses: PDF s.13.5 gives "0, 0.1, 0.5 and 2.5 ug/ml"; the data file has only 0, 0.5,

   "1:0, 4:1, 2:1, 1:1" (0:1 and 1:0 are inverses).
 - epitope mapping: PDF s.12 gives peptide at "1 mg per well" and primary at "0.1 mg per well";
   the xlsx gives "primary at 1 ug/ml" — a 100-fold discrepancy in antibody loading.

   and 8-residue sequences as "single-loop CLIPS, 17-mer" (rows 15-23), internally inconsistent

None is load-bearing, but the claim of having swept them is not met.

FINDING 20 (MINOR, logic). "Both move in the same direction, so the effect is a real increase in
cell number and not a compositional artefact of the denominator." The frequency record (FC-002,
n=13 nondiabetic donors) and the count record (FC-007, n=11 nondiabetic donors) are separate
studies on separate donor sets; no donor appears in both and neither file reports the other's
quantity. FC-007 does establish a count rise in its own donors on its own, so the conclusion is
substantively right — but "gives absolute counts on the same dose series at the same 48 hour
timepoint" invites the reading that FC-007 validates FC-002's denominator, which it cannot. This is
the same cross-record move the analysis forbids elsewhere (see Finding 3), applied here without the
caveat. Note also that recovering total CD4 requires combining the two files
(2965/0.13849 vs 2029/0.10622 = 1.121), exactly the cross-study division the analysis calls
unavailable; its hedge ("The two records together recover relative total CD4 at 48 hours") concedes
this but does not flag that the two records are different donors.

FINDING 21 (MINOR). "MDH2 reverses outright: pooled 1.2187 against paired 0.8537 at 1 of 12,
p of 0.0063" asserts significance on the estimator most favourable to it without saying so. The
sign test gives p=0.0063; the estimator the package itself prescribes (PDF s.14, paired two-tailed
t test) gives p=0.686 on raw reads and p=0.448 on log ratios, because the single DN-04 outlier
(7.394-fold up, 3037 -> 22456) dominates the mean. Wilcoxon signed-rank gives p=0.034. The
analysis elsewhere leans on the sign test to *reject* claims (ETS1, IL7R), which is consistent;
but asserting a significant reversal here, from the one test blind to the outlier it simultaneously

=====================================================================
VERDICT
=====================================================================
The analysis is arithmetically impeccable and statistically well-judged. Every one of the roughly
forty numeric claims reproduces exactly from the cells cited, the paired design is correctly
identified, all sign-test p-values are exact and correct, the pooled-versus-paired reversals are
real, and the one genuinely hard combinatorial claim (exactly one criterion-admitted transcript
fails the paired test, and it is ETS1) is right. On criteria 1, 2 and 3 of this audit I found no
errors at all.

It fails on criteria 4, 5 and 7. Three claims are asserted as if they came from the files and are
contradicted by the files: that four records share a "no-drug, no-added-agent" condition spreading
2.3330-fold (three of the four are not that condition; two records carry an added agent or an
IL-2-free zero point, so the figure is largely an IL-2 dose effect — Finding 1); that the mounted
panel contains no Treg-discriminating marker (IKZF2/Helios is present at gm 2.89, 12/12,
p=0.0005, and passes the package's own induction criterion — Finding 2); and that no timepoint
beyond 48 hours exists anywhere in the package (72 h was assessed in the cross-linking study, in a
sheet the analysis quotes for another purpose — Finding 5). Each of these three is the *sole*
stated warrant for one of the six recommended studies. One further conclusion does not follow from
its evidence at all (cross-linker independence inferred from a record in which every well contains
the cross-linker — Finding 6), and one claimed document contradiction dissolves against the PDF's
own precedence clause (Finding 4).

The conclusions themselves survive. Strike the per-cell potency claim: correct and well evidenced
(Finding 13 notwithstanding, the suppression record genuinely has no non-TRN-416 comparator).
Treat the 10-hour expression data as an acute response rather than a characterisation of the 48-hour
population: correct. Do not claim superiority over IL-2 or TNF: correct, though for the right
reason (no concurrent comparator, different donors and n) rather than the stated one. All six
proposed studies are the right studies. But three of the six rest on stated factual premises that
the room contradicts, the 21.8 percent compartment figure is quoted to three significant figures
when the room supports only a range of roughly 9 to 22 percent, and the recommendation to spend an
IND-enabling toxicology slot on a declared-clean molecule is made without once noting that the
molecule is an unmodified murine BALB/c IgG1 with no humanisation, no species cross-reactivity
data and no in vivo data anywhere in the package — the largest item in the room arguing against
its own position.

Overall: accurate in its computation, not fully accurate in its reading of the room; conclusions
supported, but three of the six study justifications and the headline variability figure need to be
rewritten before this goes to a client.
