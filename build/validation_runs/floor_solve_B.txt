INDEPENDENT READ ON THE TRN-416 PACKAGE
=======================================

RECOMMENDATION (first line, as asked)
-------------------------------------
Do not commit to IND-enabling work on TRN-416. Hold the asset at lead
optimisation and fund a bounded re-validation-and-reformatting package first:
the client's data support target engagement and a plausible TNFR2 pharmacology
signature, but they do not support a single one of the efficacy claims the
IND-enabling spend would rest on, and the molecule they have is a murine
hybridoma IgG1 that cannot be the clinical candidate, so the pivotal work
would have to be repeated on a different protein regardless. This is a hold,
not a kill: the binding package is good enough that the asset is worth a
defined, milestone-gated investment, and I set out below exactly what would
bring it back.

Short version of why: the package has one strong leg (biophysics and
selectivity), one suggestive leg (a non-canonical NF-kB / FOXP3 transcriptional
signature reproducible across 12 donors), and three legs that do not hold at
all (the only functional efficacy assay has no comparator arm; the mechanism
that would dictate the human format is untested; and several data files contain
values that cannot have arisen from the experiments as described). On top of
that the narrative report and the data files contradict each other on at least
a dozen points of fact, including the dose of test article used in one study
and the harvest time in another.


=====================================================================
PART 1. WHAT THEIR DATA DOES ESTABLISH
=====================================================================

1.1 Target engagement is real, and the biophysics is internally consistent
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx (sheet "kinetics", study TRN416-BP-009)
and TRN416_elisa_receptor_counterscreen.xlsx (sheet "counterscreen", BP-010).

- Reported global KD 1.2 nM, SE 0.2 nM (bottom rows of the kinetics sheet).
  I checked the arithmetic of the per-concentration rate constants: taking the
  50 nM row, kd/ka = 5.79e-4 / 4.82e5 = 1.201e-9 M, which reproduces the
  tabulated KD of 1.2e-09 exactly. The same holds at every concentration
  (ka 4.68e5-4.91e5, kd 5.64e-4-5.88e-4, KD 1.19-1.22 nM). The rate constants
  and the derived KD are mutually consistent. Method: divided the kd column by
  the ka column, row by row, and compared with the tabulated KD column.

- The ELISA gives an independent, concordant potency. I fitted a four-parameter
  Hill curve (scipy curve_fit) to the "TNFR2 full ectodomain OD450" column
  against the TRN-416 concentration column, with the bottom fixed at the
  0 ug/ml value of 0.047: EC50 = 0.331 ug/ml, Hill slope 0.93, top 2.657. At
  150 kDa for an IgG, 0.331 ug/ml = 2.2 nM. Two orthogonal methods agreeing
  within ~2-fold on an apparent affinity is a genuine strength.

- Selectivity against TNFR1 is clean. At the top dose of 10 ug/ml the TNFR2
  specific signal is 2.541 - 0.047 (zero-antibody well) = 2.494 OD, while the
  TNFR1 signal is 0.062 - 0.048 = 0.014 OD and the BSA signal is
  0.048 - 0.043 = 0.005 OD. Ratio of specific signals = 2.494/0.014 = 178:1,
  and the TNFR1 and BSA columns are flat across the whole 2,000-fold titration
  (TNFR1 0.048 to 0.062; BSA 0.043 to 0.048), i.e. at assay noise. Method:
  subtracted each column's own 0 ug/ml value from its 10 ug/ml value.

- The epitope is defined and the mapping is self-consistent.
  Source: TRN416_epitope_mapping_clips.xlsx (BP-011). Dividing each
  construct's "Binding (AU)" by the scrambled control (N-0001, 96 AU) gives
  signal:background of 29.9 for C-0141 (residues 44-55, QMCCSKCSPGQH), 27.7 for
  C-0207 (98-105, CSSDQVET) and 25.1 for C-0263 (116-123, NRICTCRP), against
  1.5-2.2 for the three linear peptides outside those regions (L-0088 2.0,
  L-0154 1.6, L-0301 1.5). Critically, the signal falls off correctly in the
  neighbouring frames: shifting C-0141 two residues N-terminally (C-0139,
  42-53) collapses it from 29.9 to 2.2, and shifting C-0263 two residues
  (C-0261, 114-121) drops it from 25.1 to 6.7. That frame-dependence is what a
  real discontinuous epitope looks like and is hard to fake.
  It also agrees with the counter-screen: the aa 50-122 fragment retains
  2.188/2.541 = 86% of the full-ectodomain signal at 10 ug/ml and fits an EC50
  of 0.361 ug/ml versus 0.331 ug/ml for the full ectodomain (same Hill fit as
  above, applied to the "TNFR2 aa 50-122 OD450" column) - so the epitope sits
  essentially inside the fragment used as the immunogen
  (TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf, section 1, "Immunogen").

Verdict on this leg: I would carry the binding, selectivity and epitope data
forward as supportive. It is the only part of the package I would let into a
briefing document largely as it stands.

1.2 There is a coherent, donor-reproducible transcriptional signature
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx (sheet "normalized_reads",
study TRN416-RS-001), 12 donors, paired IL-2-only vs IL-2+TRN-416 wells.

I ran the analysis the sponsor says they ran (PDF section 14, "Expression
comparisons: Paired Student's t test, two tailed"), because no statistics
appear anywhere in their files. Method: for each gene I took the 12 matched
column pairs DN-nn_IL2_only / DN-nn_IL2_plus_TRN416, computed log2(treated/
control) per donor, and ran a one-sample paired t test on those 12 ratios
(scipy.stats.ttest_1samp); the fold change quoted is the geometric mean.

The Treg identity module moves up, in the same direction in every donor:
  FOXP3   2.84-fold, p = 8.0e-9, up in 12/12 donors
  IL2RA   3.45-fold, p = 2.6e-5, n = 11 (DN-07 is blank in both arms)
  IKZF2   2.89-fold, p = 1.1e-3 (Helios)
  RUNX1   1.93-fold, p = 7.9e-4
  CD274   3.24-fold, p = 2.1e-5 (PD-L1)
  IL7R    0.52-fold, p = 0.082 (CD127 down, directionally Treg-like)

The co-activator / non-canonical signalling arm moves with it:
  EP300 3.36-fold (p = 4.8e-9), CREBBP 1.93-fold (p = 1.5e-7),
  "CREB" 1.83-fold (p = 5.0e-9).

And the independent NF-kB array points the same way.
Source: TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet "nfkb_array" (AR-012D),
n = 7, TRN-416 2.5 ug/ml vs paired IL-2-only wells. The two housekeeping genes
on the plate are not at 1.000 (RPLP0 1.124, HMBS 1.086), so I renormalised
every gene to their mean (1.105) before calling anything moved. Method:
divided each gene's "TRN-416 (RQ)" value by 1.105. Nine genes then sit at or
above 1.5-fold, and they are the right nine for TNFR2:
  TANK 2.43, LTA 2.40, RELB 2.33, BIRC3 1.90, MAP3K8 1.82, NFKBIA 1.82,
  NFKB2 1.70, NFKBIE 1.65, REL 1.55 (MAP3K14/NIK 1.47 just below).
RELB + NFKB2 + BIRC3 + NIK is the canonical non-canonical (alternative) NF-kB
readout, which is the pathway TNFR2 is supposed to drive. Two independent
platforms (RNA-seq and TaqMan array) on separate donor sets agreeing on the
pathway is the second real asset in this package.

1.3 The Treg flow signal is reproducible, dose-ordered, and present in
    patient cells
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Sources: TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx (FC-002, n=13) and
TRN416_flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors.xlsx (FC-003, n=11).

Because the files give only mean and SEM, I could not run their stated linear
mixed-effects model; I ran the most conservative test the summary statistics
permit, an unpaired Welch t test on the reported mean and SEM at
df = 2n-2 (the real design is paired, so the true p values are likely smaller).
Method: t = (mean_dose - mean_0) / sqrt(SEM_0^2 + SEM_dose^2).

  FC-002, non-diabetic, 0 vs 2.5 ug/ml: 10.622 +/- 0.706 to 13.849 +/- 1.076
    = +3.227 percentage points (+30.4% relative), t = 2.51, p ~ 0.019
  FC-003, type 1 diabetes, 0 vs 2.5 ug/ml: 10.902 +/- 0.481 to 14.368 +/- 0.680
    = +3.466 percentage points (+31.8% relative), t = 4.16, p ~ 0.0005

The curves are monotonic from 0.02 to 0.5 ug/ml and plateau from 0.5-2.5 ug/ml
to 25 ug/ml in both files, which is what a receptor-mediated effect should look
like, and the effect is at least as clean in T1D donor cells as in control
cells. Two separate donor sets, 24 donors in total, same direction, same
plateau. That is worth something.

The absolute counts move too: TRN416_treg_absolute_counts.xlsx (FC-007, n=11)
goes from 2,029 +/- 96 cells/well at 0 ug/ml to 2,965 +/- 88 at 2.5 ug/ml,
= +46.1% (computed as (2965-2029)/2029), Welch t = 7.19 - so the percentage
rise is not purely a loss of the FoxP3-negative denominator.

That is the whole of the positive case. Everything below is why it is not
enough to spend IND-enabling money on.


=====================================================================
PART 2. WHY I WOULD NOT ADVANCE IT - THE FOUR LOAD-BEARING FAILURES
=====================================================================

FAILURE 1. The only functional efficacy assay in the package has no
comparator arm, so it cannot attribute anything to TRN-416
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx (FC-008) and PDF section 8.

This is the single study that claims a therapeutically meaningful endpoint:
suppression of CD8+ division. The data do show a dose-ordered decline, from
83.66 +/- 9.71% division with no added cells to 49.41 +/- 10.73% at the "1:1"
arm (Welch t = 2.37, p ~ 0.036 on the summary statistics, df 12).

But the design cannot support the conclusion:

(a) No vehicle-expanded arm. The data file's metadata row reads "Added cells |
    CD4+ cultures expanded with TRN-416 2.5 ug/ml for 48 h, all arms", and the
    PDF confirms it: "The cells added in every ratio were expanded in this
    way." Every arm that contains cells contains TRN-416-expanded cells; the
    only cell-free arm is the 1:0 arm with 0 added cells. So the comparison
    being made is "adding CD4 cells" versus "adding no cells", not
    "TRN-416-expanded cells" versus "vehicle-expanded cells". Bulk CD4 cells
    added to an anti-CD3/IL-2 culture suppress CD8 division by IL-2 competition
    and by the ~10% Tregs they already contain, with no drug involved. The
    experiment as built cannot distinguish that from drug effect. This is not a
    nitpick - it is the one control the entire efficacy claim depends on.

(b) The ratios are mislabelled by roughly 7-fold. The file calls the columns
    "Responder:Treg ratio" and "Added cells per well", but the added cells are
    bulk CD4 cultures, which at 2.5 ug/ml are 13.85% Treg by their own FC-002
    data. At the "1:1" arm, 50,000 added cells x 0.1385 = 6,925 Tregs against
    50,000 responders, a true Treg:responder ratio of about 1:7.2, not 1:1.
    Any per-Treg potency read off this table is overstated ~7-fold.

(c) The variance swamps the effect. Converting SEM to CV at n=7
    (CV = SEM*sqrt(7)/mean): 30.7% at the 1:0 arm and 57.5% at the 1:1 arm. A
    suppression assay with a 57% coefficient of variation is not a decision-
    grade assay.

(d) The record contradicts itself on who the cells came from. PDF section 8
    says "Responders were CD8+ T cells from donors with type 1 diabetes" and,
    four rows later, "Samples (n) 7, from normal blood donors", while also
    calling the added CD4 cells "Autologous". Those three statements cannot all
    be true. If the responders and the added cells are from different donors,
    a substantial part of the CD8 division in the 1:0 arm, and of its
    suppression, is allogeneic mismatch, not pharmacology.

(e) The stated suppression index is malformed: "(Treg cell proliferation
    without Treg - Tresp proliferation with Treg) / Tresp proliferation
    without Treg cell" (PDF section 8). It subtracts two different populations'
    proliferation. No suppression index values appear anywhere in the package.

FAILURE 2. The mechanism of agonism is untested, and it is the one answer
needed before a human molecule can even be designed
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRN-416 is a mouse IgG1 kappa from a BALB/c hybridoma (PDF section 1, "Species
of origin", "Isotype"). Every detection and cross-linking reagent in the
package is anti-mouse or anti-rodent IgG (BP-010 secondary, BP-011 detection,
FC-012B cross-linker). This molecule cannot go into patients. The clinical
candidate will be a humanised or human-framework antibody, and the choice of
human Fc - IgG1 versus IgG4 versus an Fc-silent or Fc-enhanced variant - turns
entirely on whether agonism is Fc-dependent. The two studies meant to answer
that both fail:

(a) F(ab')2, study FC-012A (sheet "fab2_fragment"). The vehicle well reads
    0.16% Treg with an SEM of 0.55 at n = 5. Converting to SD:
    0.55 x sqrt(5) = 1.23, a CV of 769% on a quantity bounded at zero, which
    requires large negative percentages among the five donors. That is
    arithmetically impossible, so the control well is not a measurement. It
    also cannot be reconciled with the rest of the package: FC-012A used "Media
    as in section 4" (PDF 13.1), the same media as FC-002, whose vehicle well
    reads 10.622%. A 66-fold difference in the same nominal measurement rules
    the baseline out. Without a valid baseline, the 0.16% to 13.37% "response"
    in that sheet means nothing. (The top of the F(ab')2 curve, 13.37%, does sit
    at the same ceiling as whole IgG, 14.10% in FC-002, which weakly hints that
    agonism is Fc-independent and bivalency-driven - but across two experiments
    with a broken control, that is a hypothesis, not a finding.)

(b) Cross-linking, study FC-012B (sheet "cross_linking"). The cross-linker
    ab9165 is present in every well (PDF 13.2) and there is no zero-dose well
    and no uncross-linked parallel arm in the experiment. I compared it dose-
    for-dose against FC-002, the nearest non-cross-linked curve (method:
    matched the eight shared doses and subtracted). Cross-linking makes the
    response worse at every single dose: -1.80 pp at 0.0008, -1.29 at 0.004,
    -0.74 at 0.02, -0.05 at 0.1, -0.17 at 0.5, -1.71 at 2.5, -1.70 at 12.5,
    -0.86 at 25 ug/ml. Taken at face value, that argues against Fc-mediated
    clustering adding anything. But the comparison is cross-study (n=3 vs n=13,
    different donors), so it cannot be relied on either.

Net: after both studies, the client still does not know whether their agonist
needs an Fc. They cannot rationally pick a human isotype, which means they
cannot define the IND candidate, which means IND-enabling studies would be run
on a molecule whose pharmacology has not been established.

FAILURE 3. The Treg readout is under-specified, and the one control that
would prove selectivity was gated and never reported
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: PDF section 5 (flow panel and gating) plus all five FC flow files.

- "Treg" is defined as CD25-high, FoxP3-positive on an FL2-vs-FL1 plot, on a
  four-colour FACSCalibur using two channels. There is no viability dye, no
  CD127, no isotype control for the intracellular FoxP3 stain (quadrants are
  "set on unstained and single-stained aliquots"), and no CD4 co-stain inside
  the gate - CD4 identity rests on >95% isolation purity alone. CD25-high is
  exactly what IL-2 and TNF and any activating stimulus produce, and activated
  conventional CD4 cells transiently express FoxP3. A 3-percentage-point shift
  in a two-marker gate without a viability dye is as consistent with activation
  of conventional T cells as with Treg expansion.

- PDF section 5 explicitly defines a second gate - "population reported as
  Teff: events that are CD25 high and FoxP3 negative, assessed on the same
  plot". Those numbers exist by construction, on the same plots, and they are
  nowhere in the package. For a TNFR2 agonist, where the central safety and
  mechanism question is whether you also expand effector T cells, the absence
  of the Teff data from a package assembled for programme review is the single
  most consequential omission in these files.

- The vehicle baseline is not under control across the package. Same readout,
  same 48 h, same nominal media, same donor type (non-diabetic CD4+), zero-dose
  wells: 0.16% (FC-012A), 4.553% (FC-005), 8.66% (FC-006), 10.29% (FC-004),
  10.622% (FC-002), and 10.902% for the T1D set (FC-003). That is a 66-fold
  spread, and a 2.3-fold spread even if the impossible 0.16% is discarded.
  Every effect size in the package is a difference from one of those baselines.

- Every curve tops out in the same narrow band: 14.101% (FC-002), 14.866%
  (FC-003), 14.936% (FC-004), 13.370% (FC-012A), 12.910% (FC-012B). A common
  ceiling of ~13-15% across different agents, different donor types and
  different experiments is the signature of an assay limit, not of a shared
  pharmacological maximum. It also means the apparent non-additivity with TNF
  (FC-004 reaching 14.936% on top of saturating 20 ng/ml TNF, versus 14.101%
  for TRN-416 alone) cannot be read as evidence of a shared pathway - the
  assay had nowhere left to go.

- Effect size is not differentiated from the obvious comparators. Computed from
  their own files: TRN-416 gives +32.8% over vehicle at its best dose
  (FC-002, 10.622 -> 14.101); TNF alone gives +32.1% (FC-006, 8.660 -> 11.438);
  IL-2 alone gives +56.1% (FC-005, 4.553 -> 7.106). The drug does not out-
  perform either of the two natural ligands they tested, and low-dose IL-2 is
  the incumbent in exactly this indication. And there is no T1D advantage: at
  2.5 ug/ml the T1D and control responses are 14.368 +/- 0.680 and
  13.849 +/- 1.076, t = 0.41 - indistinguishable - so the disease rationale in
  these files is "it also works in patient cells", not "it works better".

- The absolute-count gain is partly non-selective. Dividing FC-007 counts by
  FC-002 percentages at matched doses (method: 100 x count / percent; note this
  crosses two studies, n=11 vs n=13, so treat as approximate) implies total CD4
  per well rising from 19,102 at 0 ug/ml to 21,409 at 2.5 ug/ml, +12.1%. So
  roughly a quarter of the +46.1% absolute Treg gain comes from more CD4 cells
  overall, not from a Treg-selective effect. (Separately, that implied
  19,000-21,000 CD4 cells per well is irreconcilable with PDF section 4's
  seeding density of 0.2-1 x 10^6 cells per well - a 2-10% recovery.)

FAILURE 4. Several data files contain values that cannot have come from the
experiments as described
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
I am not alleging misconduct; the likeliest explanation for most of this is
template filling and copy-paste during report assembly. But as the files
stand, these specific numbers cannot be cited, and an FDA reviewer or a
diligence team will find them.

(a) The IL-2 dose-response is not a measurement.
    Source: TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx (FC-005), n = 7, seven doses from 0
    to 5000 U/ml. The six non-zero doses read 7.106, 7.106, 7.085, 7.064,
    7.085, 7.106 with SEMs 0.319, 0.319, 0.298, 0.298, 0.298, 0.319. Across a
    2,500-fold dose range there are three distinct mean values, each repeated,
    each travelling with its own repeated SEM to three decimals. Seven
    independent donors measured under six independent conditions cannot produce
    byte-identical means and SEMs. On top of that, PDF section 4 states "All
    cell culture experiments included low-level IL-2 at 200 U/ml in the media",
    and the data file says "Doses are the quantity of IL-2 added to the well" -
    so the "0 U/ml" well actually contains 200 U/ml and the "2 U/ml" well
    contains 202 U/ml. The file therefore reports a +56% jump in Treg
    percentage (4.553 -> 7.106) from a 1% increase in IL-2. That is not
    possible. This matters beyond the file itself: FC-005 is the package's only
    IL-2 comparator, and the claim that TRN-416 acts on top of a saturating
    IL-2 signal rests on it.

(b) Three "independent donors" share identical paired values in the RNA-seq.
    Source: TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx. For SUCLG1, donors DN-01,
    DN-05 and DN-09 all read 3146.2 in the IL2_only arm and 1669.82 in the
    IL2_plus_TRN416 arm - the same pair of 6-significant-figure values, three
    times. CREBBP reads 2529.87 in both DN-01 and DN-05 IL2_only. SUCLG1 reads
    1687.41019185791 in both DN-03 and DN-11 IL2_only. Method: counted exact
    value repeats within each gene row across the 24 donor-arm columns.
    Identical 15-significant-figure values in two different donors, and
    identical value pairs in three different donors, do not arise from
    independent sequencing runs.

(c) The same file mixes incompatible provenances. Twelve cells carry 12-15
    significant figures (e.g. MYC DN-04 IL2_only = 5851.89326902868,
    PDCD1LG2 DN-04 = 52.0079388120819, SUCLA2 DN-08 = 696.106257946327) in an
    otherwise integer table. BDNF appears as two separate rows with different
    values, giving 71 rows against the 70 transcripts listed in PDF Appendix A.
    IL2RA is blank in both DN-07 arms. MDH2 in DN-04 goes 3,037 -> 22,456
    (7.4-fold up) while 11 of 12 donors go down. ETS1 falls ~5-fold in DN-03,
    DN-07 and DN-11 and rises ~2-fold in the other nine, and the gene's overall
    p value is 0.82. IL7R reads exactly 12,232 in both the DN-06 and DN-12
    treated arms.

(d) The sponsor's own significance criterion is incoherent, and would have
    failed their headline gene. PDF section 9.4: "An induction of at least
    1200 normalized reads was considered significant". That is an absolute
    read-count threshold with no direction, no fold change and no variance
    term. Applying it literally (method: per gene, counted donors whose
    treated-minus-control difference is >= +1200):
      - FOXP3 fails in 2 of 12 donors (DN-01 +865, DN-05 +822), and IKZF2 in 3
        of 12, despite both being up in every donor;
      - IL7R "passes" as an induction in 7 of 12 donors while its mean change
        is -20,570 reads, i.e. it is going down;
      - ETS1 "passes" in 9 of 12 with a mean change of +16,132 while its paired
        t test p value is 0.82;
      - 24 of the 71 rows have mean control abundance below 1,200 reads, so
        they can never meet the criterion at all.
    No gene in this package has been called by a defensible rule.

(e) Six panel genes sit at noise-level abundance, and they dominate the
    significance ranking. Mean IL2_only reads: BDNF 5.8 and 6.1, IL12B 5.9,
    GLS2 8.4, IL2 13.6, IL10 16.0. GLS2 (glutaminase 2, a liver enzyme) is the
    third most "significant" gene in the whole panel at 3.05-fold, p = 5.8e-9,
    off counts of 6-13 reads. IL12B, which CD4 T cells do not make, "falls"
    9-fold off counts of 0.1-12. Fold changes on single-digit read counts are
    not interpretable at any p value.

(f) The itaconate result has impossible precision.
    Source: TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet
    "itaconate_supernatant" (MB-012E), n = 6 donors, Metabolon scaled
    intensity. 0 ug/ml: 614,601 +/- 1,850. 2.5 ug/ml: 700,373 +/- 1,100.
    Converting SEM to CV at n=6 (SEM*sqrt(6)/mean): 0.74% and 0.38%. A
    six-donor mass-spectrometry scaled-intensity measure with sub-1%
    between-donor CV does not exist; by comparison their own flow assays run
    20-60% CV on the same kind of donor panel. The implied Welch t is 39.9 for
    a 14.0% change ((700373-614601)/614601). Separately, itaconate is made by
    ACOD1/IRG1, which is not on their 70-gene panel at all - ACO1 (+1.15-fold)
    and ACO2 (+1.43-fold) are different enzymes - so there is no transcriptional
    corroboration anywhere in the package for a metabolite claim.

(g) The soluble TNFR2 ELISA is confounded by the test article by ~4 orders of
    magnitude. Source: same workbook, sheet "stnfr2_supernatant" (EL-012C).
    Reported means: IL-2 111.2, TNF 205.9, TRN-416 323.7 pg/ml (2.91-fold over
    IL-2 alone; method: 323.7/111.2). But the matrix being measured contains
    TRN-416 at 2.5 ug/ml = 2.5 x 10^6 pg/ml, which is 7,723-fold the analyte
    signal by mass (2.5e6/323.7) - and the PDF says the dose was 12.5 ug/ml,
    which would make it 38,616-fold. An anti-TNFR2 antibody at that excess in a
    TNFR2 capture-and-detect ELISA will compete or bridge, and no spike-
    recovery or interference control is reported. The sheet also records a
    24-to-42-hour collection window and a mixture of "Commercial plates and
    custom plates", and reports no dispersion at all ("Summary statistic |
    mean"), so no statistical test is possible. This number cannot be used in
    either direction.

(h) The SPR response levels are inconsistent with the KD the same file
    reports. Source: TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx. The sheet states a
    theoretical Rmax of 26 RU, confirmed at 26 RU with 500 nM analyte. For a
    1:1 interaction, the steady-state response should be
    Rmax x C/(C + KD). Method: computed that prediction at KD = 1.2 nM for each
    analyte concentration and compared with the "Rmax observed" column.
      50 nM     predicted 25.39, reported 25.60 (ratio 1.01)
      25 nM     predicted 24.81, reported 25.10 (1.01)
      12.5 nM   predicted 23.72, reported 24.70 (1.04)
      6.25 nM   predicted 21.81, reported 24.20 (1.11)
      3.125 nM  predicted 18.79, reported 23.80 (1.27)
      1.5625 nM predicted 14.71, reported 23.10 (1.57)
    Back-solving a KD from each reported level alone (KD = C(Rmax-R)/R) gives a
    monotonic drift: 0.78, 0.90, 0.66, 0.47, 0.29, 0.20 nM - a 6-fold departure
    from the stated 1.2 nM at the bottom of the series. Either the column is
    mislabelled (in a global 1:1 fit Rmax is one shared parameter, so it should
    not vary with concentration at all) or the sensorgrams do not support the
    reported affinity. The Chi-squared column reinforces the suspicion by
    ranking perfectly with concentration (0.118, 0.104, 0.097, 0.089, 0.081,
    0.076). And the capture step as described is not physical: "Antibody was
    injected at a concentration of 1 ng/ml to reach the final capture of
    100 RU" (PDF section 10) - 100 RU of a 150 kDa IgG cannot be loaded from a
    1 ng/ml solution.

2.5 Record-versus-data contradictions (the diligence exposure)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Every one of these is a direct conflict between
TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf (TRN-DOC-0416-07 Rev 3) and the data

 1. EL-012C dose of test article: PDF 13.3 says "TRN-416 at 12.5 ug/ml"; the

 2. RS-001 harvest time: PDF 9.1 says "The incubation time for the samples
    reported here was 10 hours"; the PDF 9.2 table says "Harvest time after

 3. RS-001 normalisation scope: PDF 9.3 says the data "were normalized for each

 5. FC-008 donor attribution: "Responders were CD8+ T cells from donors with

 6. FC-008 ratios: PDF section 8 lists "Responder to Treg at 0:1, 1:1, 2:1 and

 7. MB-012E n: PDF 13.5 says "n = 6 donors" in one row and "Samples (n) 12" in

2.6 What is simply absent
-------------------------
Nothing in these files addresses: in vivo efficacy; PK or exposure; any
toxicology; species cross-reactivity (so no tox species is identified, and
since the immunogen was human TNFR2 aa 50-122 with no cross-reactivity data,
there is currently no route to in vivo proof of concept without a human-TNFR2
knock-in model, a mouse surrogate agonist, or a humanised-immune-system model);
FcgammaR binding or effector function; counter-screening against any TNFRSF
member other than TNFR1; any functional (as opposed to binding) TNFR1
counter-screen; developability (aggregation, viscosity, stability, forced
degradation) beyond ">95% by SDS-PAGE and SEC"; Treg lineage confirmation
beyond two flow markers (no CD127-low, no TSDR demethylation, no stability
after withdrawal); any protein-level confirmation of NF-kB activation (the
pathway claim is transcript-only); and any effector-T-cell or
proinflammatory-safety readout.

2.7 The safety signal their own data contains, which they have not addressed
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the part I would put in front of their board. TNFR2 agonism is not
Treg-exclusive, and their own RNA-seq says so. From the same paired analysis as
section 1.2:
  IL1B    2.37-fold up, p = 7.1e-5
  CXCR3   2.04-fold up, p = 8.0e-3
  IL2     1.90-fold up, p = 7.8e-4
  TNF     1.36-fold up, p = 2.7e-4
  TNFRSF9 1.52-fold up, p = 1.7e-2 (4-1BB, a costimulatory receptor)
  LTA     2.40-fold up on the NF-kB array after housekeeper rescaling
          (lymphotoxin-alpha)
Meanwhile the Treg effector molecules move the wrong way or not at all:
  IL10    0.18-fold, p = 3.0e-5 (a 5.5-fold decrease)
  CTLA4   0.86-fold, p = 4.8e-5
  TGFB1   1.03-fold, p = 0.16 (unchanged)
And two brakes on NF-kB are at or below the array's 0.75 reporting floor and
therefore unquantified: TNFAIP3 (A20) and TRAF3.

So the transcriptional picture is: Treg identity genes up, Treg effector genes
down or flat, inflammatory and costimulatory genes up, negative regulators of
NF-kB unmeasurable. That is a mixed signature, and for an agonist of a receptor
that is also expressed on effector T cells and on tumour-infiltrating
lymphocytes, "we raised FOXP3 but lowered IL10 and CTLA4 and raised IL1B and
LTA" is a liability question, not a supporting datum. It is also the reason the
missing Teff gate (Failure 3) is the most important gap in the package.

(Two further internal inconsistencies in the metabolic story, for
completeness: the glycolysis genes fall as a block - ALDOA 0.51, TPI1 0.68,
PFKL 0.81, PKM 0.82, ENO1 0.87, PGK1 0.89, GPI 0.90 - yet PFKFB3, the
allosteric activator of glycolysis, rises 1.54-fold; and in the TCA cycle,
citrate synthase rises 1.97-fold while almost every downstream enzyme falls
(IDH2 0.66, IDH3G 0.69, IDH3B 0.71, SDHA 0.76, MDH1 0.77, SUCLG2 0.76,
FH 0.85). That is not "a shift to oxidative metabolism"; it is a pattern that
needs explaining before it is used as supporting mechanism.)


=====================================================================
PART 3. WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE SHOWN TO BRING THE ASSET BACK, AND WHAT
WORK WOULD SHOW IT
=====================================================================

I have sequenced these so that the cheapest study that can kill the programme
comes first. Each one names what it must establish and what it must be
compared against - a comparator inside the same experiment, same donors, same
plate, same run, because cross-study comparison is what broke most of the
current package.

GATE 1 - Does TRN-416 expand Tregs selectively at all?
  Must establish: that the Treg percentage rise is Treg-selective and not
  generalised CD4 activation, and that it survives a properly specified gate.
  Compared against: within the same wells and the same acquisition, (i) a
  vehicle/isotype-matched mouse IgG1 control at the same concentration - the
  package has no isotype control anywhere; (ii) the CD25-high FoxP3-negative
  Teff gate the PDF already defines in section 5 but never reports, as a
  co-primary readout; (iii) recombinant TNF at 20 ng/ml and IL-2 at a dose on
  the steep part of its own curve, run on the same donors on the same day, so
  the "no better than TNF or IL-2" arithmetic in section 2.3 above gets a real
  answer. Add a viability dye, CD4, CD127 and a FoxP3 isotype control, and run
  it on an instrument with enough colours to do so. Pre-register the analysis,
  which must be paired per donor with the linear mixed-effects model they
  already claim in FC-002/FC-003, and must report the model output.
  Kill criterion: if the Teff gate moves as much as the Treg gate, or if the
  effect disappears against an isotype control, stop.

GATE 2 - Is the suppression real, and is it attributable to the drug?
  Must establish: that cells expanded in the presence of TRN-416 suppress
  responder proliferation more than cells expanded without it.
  Compared against: the arm the current study lacks - CD4 cells from the same
  donor expanded for the same 48 hours with vehicle or isotype control, added
  at the identical cell number per well. Also required: truly autologous
  responders (or a documented allogeneic design with its own mismatch control),
  sorted Tregs rather than bulk CD4 so the stated ratio is the actual ratio,
  and a cell-number-matched non-Treg CD4 arm to control for IL-2 competition.
  Report the suppression index with a formula that is dimensionally sound, and
  power the study to the observed variance - at the current 30-57% CV, n=7 will
  not resolve the effect.
  Kill criterion: no separation from vehicle-expanded cells at matched cell
  number.

GATE 3 - Is agonism Fc-dependent? (this gate defines the candidate)
  Must establish: whether TNFR2 agonism by this epitope requires Fc-mediated
  clustering, because that determines the human isotype.
  Compared against: all arms in one experiment, same donors, same plate -
  intact IgG, F(ab')2, monovalent Fab, F(ab')2 plus a secondary cross-linker,
  intact IgG plus cross-linker, and isotype control, each with a valid
  zero-dose well. The two current studies (FC-012A and FC-012B) each lack the
  in-experiment comparator that would make them readable, and FC-012A's
  vehicle well is arithmetically impossible.
  Why this is the gate: until it is answered, the humanised construct cannot be
  specified, and anything done before it is answered may have to be redone.

GATE 4 - Does a humanised molecule reproduce the mouse molecule's pharmacology?
  Must establish: that a humanised or human-framework antibody carrying this
  epitope, in the Fc format Gate 3 selects, matches the parent on affinity and
  on function.
  Compared against: the parental murine TRN-416 head-to-head - SPR on the same
  chip and run (target: reproduce KD 1.2 nM, and this time report sensorgrams,
  a single global Rmax, residuals, and a repeat at a higher capture level so
  the inconsistency in section 2.4(h) is resolved); the ELISA counter-screen on
  the same plate (target: reproduce EC50 ~0.33 ug/ml against the full
  ectodomain and ~178:1 selectivity over TNFR1); and the Gate 1 and Gate 2
  assays on the same donors. Add FcgammaR and C1q binding panels and
  ADCC/CDC assays, which the package entirely lacks, because an agonist that
  kills the Tregs it binds is self-defeating.

GATE 5 - Is there in vivo proof of concept, and in what system?
  Must establish: a Treg increase and a disease-relevant effect in a living
  animal, in a model where the target is actually engaged.
  Compared against: vehicle and isotype control in the same study, plus the
  standard-of-care comparator for the indication (low-dose IL-2, and
  anti-CD3 given the T1D setting). Note the design problem first: TRN-416 was
  raised against human TNFR2 and the package contains no cross-reactivity data,
  so the client must first decide and justify the system - human-TNFR2
  knock-in NOD mice, a mouse-TNFR2 surrogate agonist with matched epitope and
  format, or humanised-immune-system mice - and must show target engagement in
  whichever they choose before running efficacy.

GATE 6 - Clean up the record before anyone else reads it.
  Must establish: that the data files are the data. Specifically: retrieve the
  primary records behind FC-005 (the repeated means and SEMs), behind the
  SUCLG1/CREBBP duplicate donor values in the RNA-seq, and behind the itaconate
  SEMs; correct the 15 record-versus-data contradictions listed in section 2.5;
  reconcile the EL-012C dose and the RS-001 harvest time against lab notebooks;
  record the donor-to-sequencing-site assignment and re-normalise the RNA-seq
  with site as a covariate; and replace the "1200 normalized reads" criterion
  with a prespecified fold-change-plus-paired-test rule with multiplicity
  control, re-calling the panel under it. Repeat the sTNFR2 ELISA with a
  spike-recovery and antibody-interference control on a single plate type, or
  withdraw the result.
  This is not optional housekeeping. If the client goes to a partner or to a
  pre-IND meeting with the files as they stand, the duplicate values and the
  impossible SEMs will be found, and they will cost the programme more
  credibility than the data could ever buy it.

What I would tell the client in one sentence: pay for Gates 1-3, in that order,
with the comparators specified above; if all three read out positive, the asset
is fundable and Gate 4 onward is a normal development path; if Gate 1 or Gate 2
reads out negative, they have saved the entire IND-enabling budget.


=====================================================================
PART 4. WHAT I LOOKED AT, AND WHAT I SET ASIDE
=====================================================================

Looked at, in full: all twelve data files and the eight-page study summary. For
the record, the studies are TRN416-FC-002 through FC-008, FC-012A, FC-012B,
EL-012C, AR-012D, MB-012E, RS-001, BP-009, BP-010 and BP-011, which is the
complete list on the PDF cover page - nothing the summary references is
missing from the package.

I read every sheet whole rather than from a header row, because each .xlsx
carries a metadata block above the table; several of my findings come from that
block rather than from the data (the "all arms" admission in the suppression
file, the TRN-416 concentration in the sTNFR2 sheet, the 0.75 reporting floor
in the NF-kB array, the "doses are the quantity added" note in the IL-2 file).

Weight I placed on each piece:

  Carried as supporting, with confidence: BP-009 rate constants and derived KD
  (arithmetic self-consistent); BP-010 selectivity and EC50; BP-011 epitope
  mapping (frame-dependence is internally coherent). These are the asset.

  Carried as suggestive, needs confirmation: RS-001 FOXP3/IL2RA/IKZF2
  induction (direction consistent in every donor, so robust to the file's
  integrity problems, which affect specific cells rather than the overall
  pattern); AR-012D non-canonical NF-kB signature after housekeeper
  renormalisation; FC-002 and FC-003 dose-ordered Treg increase. I down-weighted
  all of these for the missing Teff gate, the under-specified flow panel and
  the absence of any reported statistics.

  Set aside as uninterpretable, with the reason:
   - FC-005 (IL-2 dose-response): repeated means and SEMs across six
     conditions, plus a +56% response to a 1% IL-2 increment. Not usable as the
     IL-2 comparator.
   - FC-012A (F(ab')2): vehicle well mean 0.16 with SEM 0.55 at n=5 is
     arithmetically impossible, and 66-fold off every other vehicle well under
     the same media. No valid baseline, so no dose-response.
   - FC-012B (cross-linking): cross-linker in every well, no zero dose, no
     uncross-linked arm. Nothing can be attributed to cross-linking.
   - EL-012C (soluble TNFR2): test article present at 7,700-38,600-fold the
     analyte signal with no interference control, mixed plate types, an 18-hour
     collection window, and no dispersion reported.
   - MB-012E (itaconate): sub-1% between-donor CV at n=6 on a mass-spec
     intensity, no ACOD1/IRG1 on the gene panel to corroborate it, a missing
     dose, and n stated three different ways.
   - FC-008 (suppression): no vehicle-expanded comparator; retained only as a
     design template for Gate 2, not as evidence.
   - FC-004 (TRN-416 plus TNF): n=3, and the 0 vs 25 ug/ml comparison gives
     Welch t = 1.97, p ~ 0.12 - not significant on its own summary statistics.
     Also its TNF-only well (10.29%) sits below FC-002's no-TNF vehicle well
     (10.622%), contradicting FC-006 where 20 ng/ml TNF raised Treg percentage
     by 2.16 points. Set aside as underpowered and internally inconsistent.
   - Individual RNA-seq genes at single-digit read counts (BDNF x2, IL12B,
     GLS2, IL2, IL10): excluded from any mechanistic reading, including GLS2
     despite its p = 5.8e-9, because a 6-to-17-read change carries no
     information. Note that I did include IL10's decrease in the safety
     discussion but flag it as low-abundance and therefore soft.
   - The "CREB" row: not a gene symbol (CREB1 is), so I did not interpret it
     mechanistically despite p = 5.0e-9.
   - Any cross-study quantitative comparison (FC-007 counts against FC-002
     percentages; FC-012B against FC-002) is labelled approximate where I used
     it, because donors, n and run dates differ.

  Things I deliberately did not do: I did not go outside the package. No
  literature, no competitor data, no external TNFR2 benchmarks, no reference
  sequence check on the CLIPS peptides. Every number above is computed from the
  client's own files, and the method for each is stated next to it so it can be
  re-run. Where I say something is impossible, that judgement rests on
  arithmetic internal to their files (an SEM implying negative percentages, a
  response level inconsistent with the KD in the same sheet, identical values
  in independent donors), not on an outside expectation.

  Tools used: openpyxl to read all sheets whole; scipy for the paired t tests,
  the Welch tests on reported summary statistics, the Spearman correlations and
  the Hill-curve fits; pdftotext for the study summary.
