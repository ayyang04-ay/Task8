INDEPENDENT READ — TRN-416 (anti-human TNFR2 agonist mAb), client package, assessed 13 Nov 2026

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================
Do not commit to IND-enabling work. Hold TRN-416 in lead optimisation and fund a
bounded, pre-registered go/no-go package instead. The client has convincingly
shown that TRN-416 binds TNFR2 — specifically, tightly, at a defined epitope,
with functional potency that matches its measured affinity. They have not shown
that this produces a functionally meaningful, Treg-selective effect, and the
molecule they would be IND-enabling cannot be the clinical candidate in any case.
Two findings make the spend premature rather than merely early: every stimulus
they tested, including the natural ligand, lands on the same ~13-15% Treg
ceiling, and the single study that was meant to demonstrate function was built
without the comparator arm that would have made it interpretable.

================================================================================
1. WHAT THE PACKAGE ACTUALLY ESTABLISHES (the genuinely good part)
================================================================================
These are the claims their data supports, and I would defend them.

1.1 Binding is real, tight and TNFR2-specific.
  - KD = 1.2 nM (global fit SE 0.2 nM). Source: TRN416_spr_binding_kinetics.xlsx,
    sheet "kinetics", metadata rows "Global fit KD (nM) | 1.2" and
    "Global fit KD standard error (nM) | 0.2"; corroborated by the per-
    concentration KD column, which reads 1.19-1.22e-09 M across all six analyte
    concentrations (50 down to 1.5625 nM). Method in
    TRN416_nonclinical_study_summary.pdf §10.
  - Selectivity over TNFR1 = 178-fold on specific signal. Source:
    TRN416_elisa_receptor_counterscreen.xlsx, sheet "counterscreen". Method: I
    took the 10 ug/ml row and the 0 ug/ml row for each coated antigen and
    subtracted the antigen's own 0 ug/ml OD450 from its 10 ug/ml OD450.
    TNFR2 full ectodomain 2.541 - 0.047 = 2.494; TNFR1 full ectodomain
    0.062 - 0.048 = 0.014; ratio 2.494/0.014 = 178. BSA 0.048 - 0.043 = 0.005,
    i.e. no non-specific plastic/protein binding.
  - Binding EC50 by ELISA = 0.320 ug/ml = 2.13 nM. Method: four-parameter fit
    (Hill slope fixed at 1) by scipy curve_fit to the 9-point TNFR2 full
    ectodomain OD450 column of that sheet; molar conversion using IgG MW
    150,000 g/mol (isotype IgG1 kappa, PDF §1). Concordant with the SPR KD
    within 1.8-fold.
  - The epitope is defined and is contained in CRD1-CRD2. The aa 50-122
    fragment retains 86% of full-ectodomain signal (2.142/2.494, same sheet,
    same subtraction) at an essentially identical EC50 (0.348 ug/ml = 2.32 nM).
    TRN416_epitope_mapping_clips.xlsx gives three discontinuous contact sites
    (QMCCSKCSPGQH 44-55, 2874 AU; CSSDQVET 98-105, 2655 AU; NRICTCRP 116-123,
    2413 AU) against a scrambled control of 96 AU and three non-binding linear
    peptides at 143-188 AU — a 13-30x signal-to-background.

1.2 Functional potency tracks occupancy. This is the strongest pharmacological
    argument in the package and I want it on the record.
  - Hill fits (scipy curve_fit, 4-parameter, to the full 10-point dose columns):
      TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx  EC50 0.286 ug/ml = 1.91 nM, R2 0.980
      TRN416_flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors.xlsx      EC50 0.306 ug/ml = 2.04 nM, R2 0.991
      TRN416_treg_absolute_counts.xlsx           EC50 0.167 ug/ml = 1.11 nM, R2 0.975
    Molar conversion as above. Against a measured KD of 1.2 nM, these are
    0.93x to 1.70x the KD. A functional EC50 sitting on top of the binding KD
    is the signature of genuine, target-mediated pharmacology, and it is not
    what an assay artefact looks like.

1.3 The transcriptional response is in the right pathway and is highly consistent.
  - Non-canonical NF-kB signature. Source:
    TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet "nfkb_array" (RQ vs paired
    IL-2-only wells, same donor prep, same plate, n=7): MAP3K14/NIK 1.628,
    RELB 2.573, NFKB2 1.877, BIRC3 2.099, TRAF3 at or below the 0.75 floor.
    NIK up, RelB/p52 up, BIRC3 up, TRAF3 down is textbook TNFR2 non-canonical
    signalling. Canonical arm also moves: NFKB1 1.504, NFKBIA 2.006, REL 1.710.
  - Treg identity transcripts. Source: TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx,
    sheet "normalized_reads", 12 paired donors (DN-01..DN-12, IL2_only vs
    IL2_plus_TRN416). Method: I computed per-donor log2((TRN+1)/(IL2+1)),
    reported the median across donors, ran the paired two-tailed t-test on
    log2 values that the client's own PDF §14 specifies for expression
    comparisons, and applied Benjamini-Hochberg across all 71 rows.
      FOXP3  median 2.87-fold up, 12/12 donors up, p = 8.0e-09, q = 1.4e-07
      IL2RA  median 2.86-fold up, 11/11 evaluable donors up, p = 2.6e-05
      IKZF2 (Helios) median 2.45-fold up, 12/12 up, p = 1.1e-03
    Twelve of twelve donors moving the same way on all three is a real effect,
    not noise.

1.4 The effect reproduces in the intended patient population and in absolute
    cell number, not only as a percentage.
  - Control donors (n=13): 10.622% -> 14.101% CD25hi FoxP3+ of CD4+
    (TRN416_flow_treg_dose_control_donors.xlsx, 0 vs 12.5 ug/ml rows);
    delta +3.479 points, +32.8%. Welch t-test from the tabulated mean, SEM and
    n: t = 2.68, df 20.5, p = 0.014.
  - T1D donors (n=11): 10.902% -> 14.866% (TRN416_flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors.xlsx,
    0 vs 25 ug/ml); delta +3.964, +36.4%; t = 5.17, df 19.1, p = 5.3e-05.
  - Absolute counts (n=11): 2029 -> 2965 Treg per well at 2.5 ug/ml
    (TRN416_treg_absolute_counts.xlsx), +46.1%, t = 7.19, p = 6.1e-07. So the
    percentage rise is not purely loss of the denominator.
  - Cross-check on the denominator: dividing the FC-007 absolute counts by the
    FC-002 percentages dose-by-dose gives implied total CD4+ per well of
    19,102 (vehicle) rising to 21,409 (2.5 ug/ml) — i.e. total CD4 recovery is
    flat to slightly up, so the Treg gain is not Tconv death. (Caveat: these are
    two different studies with different donors, so this is a consistency check,
    not a measurement. See §3.7 for the problem it also exposes.)

  Note on these t-tests: the studies are within-donor designs (PDF §6: "each
  donor preparation was divided across all of the conditions"), so an unpaired
  Welch test on the published mean/SEM/n is conservative. It is also the only
  test available, because per-donor values were not supplied for any flow study.

================================================================================
2. WHY THAT IS NOT ENOUGH TO FUND IND-ENABLING WORK
================================================================================

2.1 THE CEILING. Everything hits the same plateau, including the natural ligand,
    and two opposite manipulations of receptor clustering do not move it.
    Maximum CD25hi FoxP3+ (% of CD4+) observed in each arm of the package:
      14.936  TRN-416 25 ug/ml with TNF 20 ng/ml in every well (flow_treg_dose_with_tnf)
      14.866  TRN-416 25 ug/ml, T1D donors (flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors)
      14.101  TRN-416 12.5 ug/ml, control donors (flow_treg_dose_control_donors)
      13.370  F(ab')2 of TRN-416, 0.91 ug/ml — Fc REMOVED (ancillary, "fab2_fragment")
      12.910  TRN-416 25 ug/ml + anti-rodent-IgG cross-linker — Fc MULTIMERISED
              (ancillary, "cross_linking")
      11.438  TNF 100 ng/ml alone, no test article (flow_treg_tnf_dose)
       7.106  IL-2 alone, any dose from 2 to 5000 U/ml (flow_treg_il2_dose)
    Across the five antibody-containing arms the plateau is 14.04 +/- 0.90
    (mean +/- SD, CV 6.4%).
    Three consequences:
    (a) TRN-416's maximum is not statistically separable from the natural
        ligand's maximum: 14.101 +/- 1.092 (n=13) vs 11.438 +/- 0.855 (n=8),
        Welch t = 1.92, df 19.0, p = 0.070. A best-in-class engineered agonist
        that cannot beat soluble TNF on its own readout has no obvious
        therapeutic thesis over cheaper tools.
    (b) Adding TNF 20 ng/ml to every well does not raise the ceiling
        (14.936 with TNF vs 14.101/14.866 without). There is no headroom to
        recruit by co-stimulation.
    (c) This is the finding that actually governs the investment decision:
        for a TNFRSF agonist, removing the Fc should reduce the maximal
        response and Fc cross-linking should increase it, because TNFRSF
        agonism is clustering-dependent. In this package F(ab')2 reaches 13.37
        and cross-linked whole IgG reaches 12.91 — both indistinguishable from,
        and if anything below, native TRN-416 at 14.10. So either the response
        is not clustering-dependent (which undercuts the TNFR2-agonist
        mechanism) or it is already saturated by something other than the
        antibody. Either way, the standard engineering levers an IND-enabling
        programme would pull — affinity maturation, Fc engineering,
        multimerisation, hexamerisation — have no demonstrated headroom to pull
        into. Honest caveat: these are the two weakest studies in the package
        (n=5 and n=3) and they carry the heaviest inference. That is itself a
        reason to repeat them before spending, not a reason to ignore them.

2.2 THE EFFECT IS SMALLER THAN THE ASSAY'S OWN CONTROL DRIFT.
    Vehicle / no-test-article wells in nominally identical media (PDF §4: RPMI
    GlutaMAX, 10% FBS, IL-2 200 U/ml, 48 h, same gate, same instrument):
      10.902  flow_treg_dose_t1d_donors, 0 ug/ml
      10.622  flow_treg_dose_control_donors, 0 ug/ml
      10.290  flow_treg_dose_with_tnf, 0 ug/ml
       9.240  ancillary "cross_linking", lowest dose 0.0008 ug/ml
       8.660  flow_treg_tnf_dose, 0 ng/ml
       7.064  flow_treg_il2_dose, 200 U/ml IL-2 — the exact section-4 condition
       0.160  ancillary "fab2_fragment", 0 ug/ml
    Excluding the F(ab')2 sheet, the range is 7.064 to 10.902 = 3.838 points
    (mean 9.463, SD 1.452). The drug's entire effect is +3.479 (control donors)
    to +3.964 (T1D). The assay's between-study control drift is the same size as
    the signal it is being used to measure.
    Sharpest version: the IL-2 study's 200 U/ml arm (7.064 +/- 0.298, n=7) and
    the control-donor study's vehicle (10.622 +/- 0.706, n=13) are the same
    experimental condition. They differ by 3.558 points, Welch t = 4.64,
    df 15.7, p = 2.9e-04. Two vehicle-equivalent wells differ from each other
    more significantly (p = 3e-04) than drug differs from vehicle (p = 0.014).
    No IND-enabling decision should rest on an assay with that property.

2.3 THE WHOLE EFFECT SITS ON TOP OF SATURATING IL-2, AND TRN-416 WAS NEVER
    TESTED WITHOUT IT.
    TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx shows the readout is fully saturated by
    2 U/ml IL-2: 4.553 (0 U/ml) -> 7.106 (2 U/ml) -> 7.106 (5000 U/ml). The
    increment from 2 to 5000 U/ml is exactly zero. PDF §4 states every culture
    in the package contained IL-2 at 200 U/ml, i.e. 100x above saturation.
    So (i) TRN-416's +3.479 points is an increment on a maximally IL-2-driven
    system, and it is of the same order as the +2.553 points that 2 U/ml of
    generic IL-2 buys on its own; and (ii) there is no arm anywhere in the
    package — not FC-002/3/4/7/8, not RS-001 (50 U/ml IL-2, PDF §9.1), not
    FC-012A/B, not AR-012D — in which TRN-416 was given without exogenous IL-2.
    The package therefore cannot distinguish "TNFR2 agonist" from "IL-2
    co-signal amplifier". That is the mechanism question, and it is unanswered.

2.4 THE FUNCTIONAL STUDY HAS NO COMPARATOR AND CANNOT SUPPORT ITS OWN CLAIM.
    TRN416_suppression_cd8_division.xlsx, metadata row: "Added cells | CD4+
    cultures expanded with TRN-416 2.5 ug/ml for 48 h, all arms". PDF §8
    repeats it: "The cells added in every ratio were expanded in this way."
    Every arm received TRN-416-expanded cells. The titration is of cell number
    only. There is no isotype-expanded or untreated-expanded comparator, so the
    study cannot show that TRN-416 made anything more suppressive. What it
    shows is that adding CD4+ cells suppresses CD8 division — which is expected
    and is not a drug effect.
    Two further problems with the same study:
    - The added cells are bulk CD4+ cultures, not sorted Tregs, yet the ratios
      are labelled "Responder:Treg". Using the package's own Treg frequency at
      that dose (13.849% at 2.5 ug/ml, flow_treg_dose_control_donors), the
      nominal 1:1 arm (50,000 added cells against 50,000 responders) delivers
      roughly 6,924 actual Tregs — a true responder:Treg ratio of about 7.2:1,
      not 1:1. The nominal 4:1 and 2:1 arms are really about 29:1 and 14:1.
      The suppression curve is mislabelled by roughly sevenfold.
    - The effect is marginal and variable: 83.66 +/- 9.71 -> 49.41 +/- 10.73%
      CD8 division (n=7), Welch t = -2.37, df 11.9, p = 0.036 at the top arm;
      the 4:1 arm is not significant (73.01 +/- 8.52, p = 0.43).
    - PDF §8 contradicts the data file on ratios ("0:1, 1:1, 2:1 and 4:1" vs
      tabulated "1:0, 4:1, 2:1, 1:1"), contradicts itself on donors
      ("Responders were CD8+ T cells from donors with type 1 diabetes" vs
      "Samples (n) 7, from normal blood donors", while also calling them
      autologous), and states a suppression index formula —
      "(Treg cell proliferation without Treg - Tresp proliferation with Treg) /
      Tresp proliferation without Treg cell" — that is not well-formed, and no
      suppression index values are tabulated anywhere.
    Bottom line: after seven donors and a four-point titration, the package
    contains no measurement of TRN-416's effect on Treg function.

2.5 THE TRANSCRIPTIONAL DATA SHOW IDENTITY WITHOUT THE SUPPRESSIVE PROGRAMME,
    AND AN INFLAMMATORY SIGNATURE ALONGSIDE IT.
    Same paired analysis as §1.3, TRN416_expression_normalized_reads.xlsx,
    n=12 paired donors, median per-donor fold change, paired t-test on log2,
    BH-adjusted:
      UP, identity:   FOXP3 2.87x (12/12), IL2RA 2.86x (11/11), IKZF2 2.45x (12/12)
      DOWN, effector: IL10  0.21x (12/12 donors down, p = 1.9e-05, q = 6.1e-05)
                      CTLA4 0.87x (12/12 down, p = 4.8e-05)
                      PDCD1 0.80x (12/12 down, p = 4.7e-04)
      FLAT:           TGFB1 1.03x (8 up / 4 down, p = 0.16, not significant)
      UP, inflammatory: IL1B 1.98x (12/12), TNF 1.37x (12/12), IL2 1.47x (12/12),
                      CD274 3.28x (12/12), PDCD1LG2 1.79x (12/12)
      DOWN, Th2/Th1:  IL4 0.24x (12/12), IL12B 0.28x (12/12)
    IL-10 is the single most consistent change in the entire dataset — a
    five-fold reduction, in every donor — and it points the wrong way. The three
    canonical Treg suppressive effectors (IL-10, TGF-beta, CTLA-4) are
    respectively down five-fold, unchanged, and down. A cell that gains FOXP3,
    CD25 and Helios while losing IL-10 and CTLA-4 and gaining IL-1beta and TNF
    is at least as consistent with activation-induced FOXP3/CD25 in conventional
    CD4 cells as with induction of a functional Treg. That hypothesis is exactly
    what §2.4 fails to exclude and what §2.6 fails to exclude.
    This is not a fatal result — transcript is not protein and 10 h (or 1 h, see
    §3.2) is early — but it means the central biological claim is unproven in
    the direction that matters, and it raises a specific autoimmunity liability
    (TNF and IL-1beta transcript induction in CD4 cells of T1D patients).

2.6 THE SPECIFICITY CONTROL WAS DEFINED, COLLECTED, AND NEVER REPORTED.
    PDF §5 defines two gates on the same FL2/FL1 plot: "Gating, population
    reported as Treg — CD25 high and FoxP3 positive" and "Gating, population
    reported as Teff — CD25 high and FoxP3 negative". The Teff gate appears in
    no data file in the package. The one measurement that would separate
    "selective Treg expansion" from "general CD4 activation" exists in the raw
    FCS files and has not been shown. Nor is there any Treg-confirmatory marker
    anywhere: no CD127-low, no Helios protein, no FOXP3 TSDR demethylation, no
    naive/memory split. The entire Treg claim rests on two colours
    (PE-CD25 in FL2, AF488-FoxP3 in FL1) on a four-colour FACSCalibur, at 48 h,
    in 200 U/ml IL-2 — conditions under which FOXP3 and CD25 are both
    activation-inducible in conventional CD4 cells.
    Separately: there is no isotype control in any dose-response. "Dose 0" is
    a no-antibody well, not a matched mouse IgG1 well.

2.7 THE AGONIST DRIVES SHEDDING OF ITS OWN TARGET, AND THAT MEASUREMENT IS
    ITSELF UNCONTROLLED.
    TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, sheet "stnfr2_supernatant", n=10:
    sTNFR2 is 111.2 pg/ml with IL-2 alone, 205.9 with TNF, 323.7 with TRN-416 —
    2.91-fold over IL-2 control and 1.57-fold over the natural ligand (ratios
    computed from the tabulated means). Soluble TNFR2 is a decoy; an agonist
    that raises its own soluble receptor by threefold creates a target-mediated
    sink, non-linear PK, and a plausible mechanistic explanation for the ceiling
    in §2.1. That is a real development question and it is nowhere addressed.
    It is also not safely measurable as reported: the assay is a TNFR2-capture
    ELISA (metadata: "custom plates coated with 2 ug per well anti-TNFR2") run
    on supernatant that contains TRN-416, an anti-TNFR2 antibody, at 2.5 or
    12.5 ug/ml. TRN-416 can block or bridge the capture. No antibody-spiked
    recovery control is described. No SEM, SD or p-value is reported for any of
    the three conditions. And the PDF (§13.3) states the test article was at
    12.5 ug/ml while the data file header states 2.5 ug/ml.

2.8 THE MOLECULE IN HAND CANNOT BE THE CLINICAL CANDIDATE.
    PDF §1: "Species of origin — Mouse. Hybridoma raised in BALB/c."
    "Isotype — IgG1, kappa." Confirmed throughout by the detection reagents:
    anti-mouse/anti-rodent IgG secondary in the ELISA (§11), the CLIPS mapping
    (§12), and the cross-linking reagent ab9165 (§2, §13.2).
    A murine IgG1 hybridoma product cannot be dosed chronically in type 1
    diabetes. It must be humanised or replaced. Humanisation re-frameworks a
    discontinuous CRD1/CRD2 epitope and routinely shifts affinity and
    fine-specificity; and because mouse IgG1 has no meaningful human effector
    function, the Fc-dependent experiments (F(ab')2, cross-linking) do not
    translate at all. IND-enabling work would therefore be funded on a molecule
    whose pharmacology package must be regenerated from scratch on the actual
    candidate. That alone argues for sequencing the spend differently.

2.9 THERE IS NO IN VIVO DATA, AND NO T1D-SPECIFIC RATIONALE IN THE PACKAGE.
    - The whole package is in vitro. PDF front matter cites "Animal use protocol
      IACUC-2016-041" and §1 describes murine immunisation, but no in vivo
      pharmacology, PK, or tolerability study is included or referenced in the
      study list (FC-002 to FC-008, FC-012A/B, EL-012C, AR-012D, MB-012E,
      RS-001, BP-009/010/011). Either those studies do not exist or they were
      not supplied; both are worth asking about directly.
    - The T1D thesis is not supported by their own data. Baseline Treg% in T1D
      donors (10.902 +/- 0.481, n=11) is indistinguishable from control donors
      (10.622 +/- 0.706, n=13): Welch t = 0.33, df 20.4, p = 0.75. The TRN-416
      effect is not larger in T1D (max 14.866 +/- 0.597 vs 14.101 +/- 1.092;
      t = 0.62, df 18.3, p = 0.55). So the package shows neither a Treg deficit
      to correct nor preferential responsiveness in the target population.
    - Selectivity was tested against exactly one other receptor (TNFR1) plus
      BSA. For a CRD-directed antibody there is no counterscreen against the
      structurally related TNFRSF members — several of which (TNFRSF9, TNFRSF18,
      TNFRSF25, CD40) are on the client's own RNA panel — and no tissue
      cross-reactivity or membrane-proteome screen.
    - TNFR2 agonism carries a known oncology liability (TNFR2 on tumour-
      infiltrating Tregs and MDSCs). Nothing in the package addresses it.

================================================================================
3. RECORD-QUALITY DEFECTS (these change how much weight the data can carry)
================================================================================
I would raise all of these with the client; several are correctable on paper.

3.1 The RNA-seq "significance" rule is not a statistical test.
    PDF §9.4: "An induction of at least 1200 normalized reads was considered
    significant." That is an absolute count threshold — no fold change, no
    variance, no p-value, no multiple-testing control — and it mechanically
    favours highly expressed genes. Applying it to their own file (mean of the
    12 IL2-only columns vs mean of the 12 TRN-416 columns): FOXP3 passes
    (+3,125 reads), IL2RA passes (+7,159), IKZF2 passes (+2,830) — but IL10
    (-12.9 reads), CTLA4 (-166), IL4 (-332), IL12B (-5.1), IL1B (+373) and TNF
    (+141) all fail. The rule would discard the five-fold, 12-of-12-donor IL-10
    loss that is the most consistent finding in the dataset and the one that
    undercuts the thesis. My analysis in §1.3/§2.5 used the paired t-test the
    client specifies in PDF §14 for expression comparisons, plus BH correction,
    which is the defensible method and which they already own.
    Also: only 70 transcripts are reported (PDF Appendix A) out of whole-
    transcriptome sequencing, so no transcriptome-wide claim can be made.

3.2 The RNA-seq timepoint is self-contradictory. PDF §9.1: "The incubation time
    for the samples reported here was 10 hours." PDF §9.2, table:
    "Harvest time after addition of test article — 1h". These cannot both be
    true. Ten hours and one hour imply very different biology.

3.3 The RNA-seq has an unrecorded batch split that is visible in the data.
    PDF §9.3: libraries were built at two sites with two different kits
    (NEBNext Ultra Directional at Calder, Ultra II at Westmoor) and "normalized
    for each sequencing laboratory separately"; PDF §9.4 then says "Raw reads
    were normalized for subjects separately". Those are different statements,
    and neither site nor batch is recorded in the data file. The consequence is
    directly visible:
      IL7R: seven donors show +0.19 to +0.79 log2FC; five donors (DN-06, 07,
      09, 11, 12) all collapse from ~80,000-99,000 to ~11,000-12,000, i.e.
      log2FC -2.71 to -2.97. That is discrete bimodality, not biological spread.
      ETS1: nine donors +0.75 to +1.15; three donors (DN-03, 07, 11) at -2.34
      to -2.37. The paired t-test on ETS1 is p = 0.83 — statistically null
      purely because of the split.
    Until site is supplied as a covariate, individual gene calls from this file
    should be treated as provisional. (The headline calls in §1.3 and §2.5 are
    robust to it: they are 11-12/12 consistent with median-based effect sizes.)

3.4 Data-integrity marks in the RNA-seq file.
    - 71 data rows for the 70 transcripts of Appendix A: BDNF appears twice,
      with different values (median FC 0.53 and 0.77). Which row is the record
      is unknown.
    - IL2RA is blank for DN-07 in both arms.
    - "CREB" is not a valid HGNC symbol (CREB1).
    - Identical values repeated across donors within the same arm, e.g. SUCLG1
      IL2-only = 3146.2 in DN-01, DN-05 and DN-09 and SUCLG1 TRN-416 = 1669.82
      in the same three; SUCLG1 IL2-only = 1687.41019185791 in both DN-03 and
      DN-11; CREBBP IL2-only = 2529.87 in DN-01 and DN-05. These look like
      copy-fill, not independent measurements.
    - Twelve cells carry 15 significant figures (e.g. MYC DN-04 IL2-only =
      5851.89326902868) in a file otherwise populated with integers — mixed
      provenance.
    - MDH2 DN-04 TRN-416 = 22,456 against its own control of 3,037 and a
      range of 1,113-4,132 in every other donor: a 7.4-fold single-donor
      outlier that flips the mean fold change to 1.22 while 11 of 12 donors
      go down. (I used medians throughout, so this did not affect my calls.)

3.5 The IL-2 dose-response table is not credible as six independent measurements.
    TRN416_flow_treg_il2_dose.xlsx: the six non-zero doses (2 to 5000 U/ml,
    a 2,500-fold range, n=7 donors) yield only three distinct means — 7.064,
    7.085, 7.106 — and two distinct SEMs, locked in fixed pairs (7.106 always
    with 0.319; 7.085 and 7.064 always with 0.298). Exact repetition to three
    decimals across seven independent donors is not what measurement produces.
    The qualitative conclusion I drew from it in §2.3 (the readout is saturated
    well below 200 U/ml) is probably right, but the table should be re-derived
    from source before anyone cites it.

3.6 The F(ab')2 study is not comparable to anything else in the package.
    TRN416_ancillary_functional_assays.xlsx, "fab2_fragment": the vehicle well
    reads 0.16% +/- 0.55 SEM (n=5). The SEM is 3.4 times the mean, implying an
    SD of 1.23 and a distribution that extends well below zero — impossible for
    a percentage. The same vehicle condition reads 10.622% in FC-002: a 66-fold
    discrepancy. The curve is also non-monotonic, peaking at 13.37% at
    0.91 ug/ml and falling to 8.24% at 18.3 ug/ml (-38% from peak). I used this
    study only for its plateau value in §2.1, and flagged the weakness there.

3.7 Smaller inconsistencies, each individually minor.
    - Cross-linking study (FC-012B) has no 0 ug/ml arm at all, so the effect of
      cross-linking cannot be assessed within the study; SEM is reported for
      only four of eight doses; n=3; and 24 h and 72 h timepoints were collected
      (PDF §13.2) but not tabulated.
    - Itaconate (MB-012E): PDF §13.5 specifies doses 0, 0.1, 0.5 and 2.5 ug/ml;
      the data file has only 0, 0.5 and 2.5. PDF says "n = 6 donors" in one row
      and "Samples (n) 12" in the next. The change is +14.0% (614,601 ->
      700,373 scaled intensity) with SEMs of 0.30% and 0.16% of the mean at
      n=6 — dispersion that tight across six human donors indicates technical,
      not biological, replicates. And the enzyme that makes itaconate
      (ACOD1/IRG1) is not on the 70-gene panel, so there is no transcriptional
      corroboration.
    - NF-kB array (AR-012D): the two housekeeping genes return RQ values of
      1.124 (RPLP0) and 1.086 (HMBS) rather than 1.000, so the RQ scale carries
      an 8.6-12.4% positive offset before any gene is read. Four genes (MAP3K1,
      MAP3K12, TNFAIP3, TRAF3) are reported exactly at the stated 0.75 lower
      limit and are censored values, not measurements. Twenty-one of 37
      non-housekeeping genes fall between 0.75 and 1.5. No SD, SEM or p-value
      is given for n=7. There is also an internal inconsistency: NFKBIA (IkB-
      alpha) is 2.006 while TNFAIP3 (A20) is at the floor — both are direct,
      co-regulated NF-kB target genes and should move together.
    - Epitope mapping: the tabulated peptide sequences do not match the stated
      construct lengths. "Double-loop CLIPS, 22-mer" rows carry 12-residue
      sequences; "single-loop CLIPS, 17-mer" rows carry 8-residue sequences;
      "15-mer linear" rows carry 16-residue sequences. PDF §12 also states
      peptide loading of "1 mg per well" and primary antibody at "0.1 mg per
      well", roughly a thousandfold off plausible ELISA quantities and
      contradicted by the data file's own "primary at 1 ug/ml". Two of the three
      claimed contact sites (44-55 and 116-123) extend outside the aa 50-122
      fragment that retains 86% of binding. And 13 constructs are reported from
      a library of "up to 10,000" with no stated positivity cutoff.
    - SPR: the fitted Rmax falls monotonically with analyte concentration
      (25.6 RU at 50 nM down to 23.1 RU at 1.5625 nM, -9.8%) while the capture
      level is constant within 1% (99.5-101.4 RU). Regression of Rmax on
      log10[analyte]: slope +1.60 RU/decade, r = 0.997, p = 1.8e-05. For a true
      1:1 Langmuir interaction Rmax is a property of the surface and must be
      concentration-independent; a concentration-dependent Rmax indicates the
      1:1 global fit is absorbing an avidity or mass-transport term. The KD is
      probably still about right (it is stable at 1.19-1.22 nM across a 32-fold
      analyte range, and the ELISA EC50 of 2.1 nM agrees independently), but
      the kinetic constants (ka ~4.8e5, kd ~5.8e-4) should not be quoted as
      definitive. Note also the reported theoretical Rmax of 26 RU is below the
      ~35 RU a bivalent 100-RU IgG surface would predict for a ~26 kDa analyte,
      so the surface may be partly inactive — which is consistent with an
      avidity-contaminated fit.

================================================================================
4. NEXT STEPS — WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE SHOWN TO BRING THE ASSET BACK
================================================================================
Since my read is that it should not advance now, here is the work that would
reverse that, with the comparator each study must run against and the bar it
must clear. I would pre-register every bar before the first experiment, and
I would sequence them so the cheapest killing experiments come first.

TIER 1 — the three experiments that decide the asset (run these first; they are
cheap, they use the murine clone, and two of them can kill the programme).

Study A. Qualify the primary pharmacology assay.
  Must establish: the run-to-run and donor-to-donor variance of the CD25hi
  FoxP3+ readout, and the minimum difference the assay can actually detect.
  Compare against: a stated vehicle acceptance range and an in-assay reference
  standard run on every plate. Include a matched mouse IgG1 isotype arm — the
  package has never run one.
  Bar to clear: the vehicle-corrected TRN-416 effect must exceed three times
  the qualified vehicle SD. On current data it does not: the effect is 3.5-4.0
  points and the vehicle SD across nominally identical conditions is 1.45
  points (§2.2), giving a ratio of ~2.4.
  If this fails, nothing downstream is interpretable and the programme stops
  here for a fraction of the cost of a tox study.

Study B. Is the induced population a Treg, or an activated Tconv?
  Must establish: that TRN-416 raises Tregs selectively, not CD25/FOXP3 on
  conventional CD4 cells.
  Compare against: (i) matched mouse IgG1 isotype, same donor, same plate;
  (ii) the CD25hi FoxP3-negative Teff gate that PDF §5 already defines and that
  no file reports — this costs nothing, the data are in their existing FCS files
  and should be requested before any new work is commissioned.
  Readouts to add: CD127-low, Helios protein, FOXP3 TSDR demethylation on
  sorted CD4+CD25hi cells, naive/memory split, and a time course beyond 48 h to
  show whether the phenotype is durable or transient-activation.
  Bar: the Treg:Teff ratio rises at least 1.5-fold over isotype, and the
  TSDR-demethylated fraction increases. If FOXP3 induction is TSDR-methylated
  and the Teff gate moves with it, the asset is an activation signal, not a Treg
  agonist, and it stops.

Study C. Is the ceiling the antibody's or the assay's?
  Must establish: whether anything can push the response above ~15%.
  Compare against: all in one experiment with one shared vehicle and isotype —
  TNF across its full range (the natural ligand, as full-agonist reference), a
  second independent anti-TNFR2 agonist clone as benchmark, TRN-416 native,
  TRN-416 F(ab')2, TRN-416 plus cross-linker, and a multimerised/hexamerised
  format. This repeats FC-012A and FC-012B properly: adequately powered (the
  current versions are n=5 and n=3), with a true 0 ug/ml arm (FC-012B has none),
  and in one run so the §2.2 drift cannot confound the comparison.
  Bar: at least one manipulation must exceed the ~14-15% plateau, or the
  plateau must be shown to be imposed by the assay (e.g. by gate saturation or
  culture duration) rather than by the biology. If the ceiling is real and
  immovable, there is no engineering programme to fund and the asset should be
  terminated regardless of how good the binding is.

TIER 2 — only if Tier 1 clears.

Study D. Does TRN-416 do anything to Treg function?
  Must establish: that Tregs from TRN-416-treated cultures suppress better than
  Tregs from isotype-treated cultures.
  Compare against: isotype-expanded Tregs from the same donor, at MATCHED
  SORTED-TREG INPUT (not matched bulk CD4 — §2.4 shows the current ratios are
  about sevenfold out), plus a no-added-cell control and a Treg-depleted bulk
  CD4 control to exclude simple cell crowding and IL-2 competition.
  Add protein readouts where the transcripts go the wrong way: IL-10 and
  TGF-beta by ELISA/intracellular stain, CTLA-4 by flow. §2.5 found IL10 down
  5-fold and CTLA4 down in 12 of 12 donors; if the protein agrees, the asset
  induces a phenotype without a function.
  Bar: a suppression advantage of at least 25 percentage points over
  isotype-matched sorted Tregs at equal Treg input, and no reduction in IL-10
  or CTLA-4 protein.

Study E. Agonist, or IL-2 co-signal?
  Must establish: whether TRN-416 does anything in the absence of exogenous
  IL-2 — a question the package has never asked (§2.3).
  Compare against: a factorial design, TRN-416 full dose-response crossed with
  IL-2 at 0, 2 and 200 U/ml, with the IL-2-alone dose-response (FC-005) re-run
  from source in the same experiment because the current table is not credible
  as independent data (§3.5).
  Bar: a dose-dependent TRN-416 effect at 0 U/ml IL-2. If the effect requires
  saturating IL-2, the product profile is "adjunct to low-dose IL-2", which is
  a different asset with a different competitor set and should be priced as such.

Study F. Selectivity and the shedding liability.
  Must establish: (i) that TRN-416 does not engage other TNFRSF CRD proteins,
  and (ii) whether the threefold sTNFR2 rise is real and what it does to
  exposure.
  Compare against: a TNFRSF panel (TNFR1, TNFRSF9, TNFRSF18, TNFRSF25, CD40,
  OX40) plus a membrane-proteome counterscreen and human tissue cross-
  reactivity. For the shedding question, repeat EL-012C with a TRN-416-spiked
  recovery control and a capture antibody shown not to compete with TRN-416,
  with SD and n reported (§2.7), and resolve the 2.5 vs 12.5 ug/ml discrepancy
  between the PDF and the data file.
  Bar: no engagement of other TNFRSF members; and sTNFR2 induction either
  disproven or shown not to produce a target-mediated sink at projected
  exposures.

TIER 3 — candidate and in vivo. Do not start before Tier 1 clears, and do not
treat Tier 1/2 data on the murine clone as transferable.

Study G. Make the actual candidate. Humanise or re-derive as a human IgG;
  confirm the CRD1/CRD2 discontinuous epitope and the 1.2 nM affinity survive,
  by SPR (with a corrected fit — see §3.7) and by the CLIPS/fragment ELISA;
  then repeat Studies A-F on the clinical molecule. Compare against: the murine
  parental at every step. Bar: KD within twofold and the same epitope.

Study H. In vivo proof of concept. Human TNFR2 knock-in or humanised-mouse T1D
  model. Compare against: isotype control AND an active comparator — low-dose
  IL-2, since their own FC-005 shows IL-2 alone produces effects of the same
  order (§2.3) and any reviewer will ask. Readouts: Treg:Teff ratio in blood
  and pancreatic lymph node, insulitis score, and a disease endpoint, not a
  biomarker alone. Bar: disease modification beating isotype and at least
  matching the IL-2 arm.

STOP RULE. If Study C cannot move the ceiling and Study D cannot show a
suppression advantage over isotype at matched Treg input, terminate. Those two
results together would mean the asset binds its target beautifully and does
nothing distinctive with it, and no amount of protein engineering fixes that.

TWO THINGS TO ASK THE CLIENT BEFORE SPENDING ANYTHING.
  1. The Teff gate (PDF §5) already exists in their archived FCS files for every
     flow study. Request it. It is free and it may answer Study B on its own.
  2. PDF front matter cites animal use protocol IACUC-2016-041 but the package
     contains no in vivo study (§2.9). Ask what exists and why it was not
     included.

  - The SPR kinetic rate constants ka and kd. The KD is defensible; the rate

METHODS NOTE. All statistics were computed by me from the tabulated summary
values or the per-donor matrix, using Python with scipy. For the flow studies
only mean, SEM and n are supplied, so group comparisons are two-sample Welch
t-tests reconstructed from those three numbers; because the designs are
within-donor (PDF §6), these are conservative. Dose-response EC50s are
four-parameter Hill fits (scipy.optimize.curve_fit) to the full tabulated dose
columns, with the zero-dose point set to 1e-6 ug/ml to permit the log fit. The
RNA-seq analysis is per-donor log2((TRN-416 + 1)/(IL-2-only + 1)), summarised as
the across-donor median, tested by paired two-tailed t-test on log2 values (the
test the client specifies in PDF §14), with Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment across
all 71 rows; DN-07 is excluded for IL2RA only, where both cells are blank.
